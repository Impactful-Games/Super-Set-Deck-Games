= Dead Drop
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 13 Information cards:
** 0 in ♠, ♥, ♣ & ♦.
** 1 in ♠, ♥ & ♣.
** 2 in ♠ & ♥.
** 3 in ♠ & ♥.
** 4 in ♠.
** 5 in ♠.
* 4 additional cards for the advanced game:
** 1 in ♦.
** 2 & 3 in ♣.
** 4 in ♥.
****

As secret agents working for different organizations, you scour the globe for information that will help you find the location of a hidden explosive device.
Agents must trade information and sell secrets to learn the location of the device, and grab it before it falls into the hands of another agency!

Dead Drop is a game of memory, deduction and crafty maneuvering.

[CAUTION]
====
For this game, the suits of the cards do not matter.
Only their values count.
====


== Setup

Shuffle the deck and place 1 card face down in the middle of the table.
This is *the Drop*: the card represents the information you must obtain to locate the explosive device.

Place as many cards as there are players face up in a row on the side of the table, to form *the Stash*.

Deal the remaining cards evenly among the players:

* 4 players: 2 cards each.
* 3 players: 3 cards each.
* 2 players: 5 cards each.


== Actions

On your turn, you must perform 1 of the following actions:

* *Share Info:* +
Trade *1* card from your hand with *1* card of any other player.
The target player *must* trade, and *may not* look at the card you give them before handing you their card.

* *Swap the Stash:* +
Trade *1* card from your hand with one card of the Stash.
Place the card from your hand face up: it becomes part of the Stash.

* *Sell Secrets:* +
Reveal 2 cards from your hand to another player.
They must answer *"yes"* or *"no"*, revealing whether or not they have a card equal to the sum of these cards.
If they answer *"yes"*, they must hand you a card of that value, and you must choose *1* of your revealed cards to give them in return.


== End of turn

After your action, you may choose to take the following action before passing your turn:

* *Grab the Drop:* +
Place *2* cards from your hand face up next to the Drop, then secretly look at the Drop.
If the Drop is equal to the sum of the 2 cards you placed, you win the round!
If not, you are eliminated from the round.

IMPORTANT: When you try to grab the Drop, the ♠5 may count as 0.

In a 3- or 4-player game, when a player is eliminated, place their cards face up with the other cards of the Stash.
If only one player is left, they win the round.
In a 2-player game, when a player is eliminated, the other player wins the round.


== End of the game

The first player to win *3* rounds wins the game!


== Advanced game

For an added challenge, add the cards of the advanced game.

Each player receives one more card:

* 4 players: 3 cards each.
* 3 players: 4 cards each.
* 2 players: 6 cards each.

Furthermore, the Stash always contains *4 cards*, whatever the number of players.


== Two-player variant

With two players, the first player may be able to deduce the Drop at the end of their first turn.
To prevent this, you can apply the following variant, which slows the two-player game down a bit:

* Each of the two players receives one card less.
* Place 2 cards face down next to the Drop: this is *the Middleman*.
* You can *Share Info* with the Middleman, by trading *1* card from your hand face down with one of these two cards.
