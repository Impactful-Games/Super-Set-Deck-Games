= Hungry Monkey
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 72 cards: 1-11 & K in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿ & ★.
****

In Hungry Monkey, you have cards both in hand and in a face-down row in front of you.
*In order to win, you must be the first to play all your cards*, first from your hand and then from your card row.

CAUTION: For this game, the suits of the cards *do not* matter: only their values do.


== Setup

. Shuffle all the cards and deal 3 to each player as their hand.
. Deal *4 cards, face down in a row*, to each player.
No one is allowed to look at these cards unless a card effect allows it later in the game.
. Place the remaining cards in the middle of the playing area as a face-down draw pile.
. Without looking at them, remove a number of cards from the draw pile depending on the number of players:
+
[%autowidth, cols=">,^,^,^"]
|===
h| Number of players | 2 | 3 | 4
h| Cards removed | 24 | 16 | 8
|===
+
With 5 or 6 players, play with the whole draw pile.

The youngest player starts.


== Gameplay

Players take turns playing cards on the central pile, in clockwise order.
On your turn, you *must*:

. Play *one or more* cards face up on the central pile.
. Trigger a *gang of four* if possible, *otherwise* trigger the *card effect*, if any.
. *Draw back up to 3 cards* in hand, if possible.

*During your turn, you try to:*

* Optimize your cards (row and hand) until the draw pile is empty.
* Get rid of all your cards after the draw pile is empty.


=== 1. Play cards

Choose one of these ways to play cards:

* *Play cards from your hand*: +
Play *any number of identical valid cards* from your hand.
+
NOTE: You can *never play invalid cards* from your hand.

* *Play 1 card from the draw pile*: +
Draw the top card of the draw pile and play it face up on the central pile.
+
NOTE: You may choose to play a card from the draw pile even if you have a valid card in hand.

* *Play 1 card from your card row*: +
If, and only if, the draw pile and your hand are empty, you may play *only one* card from your card row on the central pile.

*Valid cards*: +
For a card to be valid on the central pile, it must have the *same or a higher number* than the card on top of the central pile.
If the *central pile is empty*, any card is valid.

*Invalid cards*: +
When you play a card from the draw pile or from your card row, it may show a lower number, and is therefore invalid.
In this case, you *must* add the central pile, including the card you played, to your hand.
*Do not* trigger its effect.

*Card effects may change these rules!*

.Playing from the draw pile
====
You play a card from the draw pile: a 3.
It has a lower number than the 10 on the central pile, which makes it invalid.
Add the whole central pile to your hand.
*Do not* trigger the effect of the 3.
====

IMPORTANT: If the draw pile is empty and you have cards in hand, but no valid card, you must add the central pile to your hand.
Then your turn is over.


=== 2. Trigger an effect

*Only* the topmost card you played can trigger a *card effect*.

If you play several cards of the same value, apply the effect only once.

A *gang of four* (see below) prevents any card effect!

.Playing from your hand
====
You play two 2s.
The card effect is triggered only *once*.
====


==== Card effects

* *1*:
** A 1 is *always valid*.
** If you play your 1 on another 1, nothing happens.
** If you play your 1 on another card, *take all* the cards of the central pile, except your 1.

* *2*:
** *Swap* one of your face-down cards with one of the cards in your hand.
** If your hand is empty, the 2 has no effect.

* *3*:
** *Peek* at one face-down card of any player, including yourself.

* *7*:
** Take an *extra turn* (*after* the draw step).

* *8*:
** The next player must play a card with the *same or a lower* number.

* *11*:
** Discard all the cards of the central pile, including these 11s, to the discard pile.

* *K*:
** A K is *always valid*.
** A K can be played alone or together with other valid cards.
** If another card is on top of the K, the K copies its effect.
** If no other card is on top of the K, the K copies the card below it and triggers its effect.
** The copy of a K is permanent and does not need to be triggered.


==== A gang of four

If there are ever four or more cards of the same value (including copies by a K) on top of the central pile, the current player *must*:

. Immediately discard all the cards of the central pile to the discard pile, *without* triggering any card effect.
. Draw back up to 3 cards.
. Take another turn.

[CAUTION]
====
A K always counts towards a gang of four.
It does not matter which card it copied originally.
====

.A gang of four
====
You play three 9s.
Together with the K on top of the central pile, they trigger a gang of four.
====


=== 3. Draw back up to 3 cards

Draw until you have 3 cards in hand (as long as the draw pile is not empty).

If you have 3 or more cards in hand, do not draw.


== End of the game

*The first player to play all their cards*, both from their hand and from their card row, *is the winner*.

Play continues with the remaining players until only one player has cards left.
That player loses.


== Optional scoring

Score over 4 games.

For each game:

* The first player to play all their cards scores 5 points.
* The second player to play all their cards scores 3 points.
In a 2-player game, the second player does not score.
* The third player to play all their cards scores 2 points.
In a 3-player game, the third player does not score.
* The last player with cards left loses 1 point.
If they have no points, they lose none.

The player who lost the last game starts the next one.

After 4 games, the player with the most points wins.
In case of a tie, all tied players win.
