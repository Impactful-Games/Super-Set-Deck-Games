****
* Base game:
** 5 A cards: A in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦ & ★.
** 60 playing cards:
*** 45 Expedition cards: 2-10 in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦ & ★.
*** 15 Wager cards: the 3 K in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦ & ★.
* _Six-expedition variant_:
** 1 additional A card: ✿A.
** 12 additional playing cards:
*** 9 Expedition cards: 2-10 in ✿.
*** 3 Wager cards: the 3 K in ✿.
* Paper and a pencil to keep the score.
****

*The Duel of the Explorers*

Place your cards to form expedition routes that lead you to remote and mysterious corners of the Earth: the Himalayan mountains, the Central American rainforest, the Egyptian desert, a mysterious volcano and the bottom of the sea.
And if you are particularly daring, you can also bet on the success of an expedition with Wager cards.
But beware: this can backfire.
If after three rounds you have the highest overall score, you win.

NOTE: The game rules are very simple.
But don't get the wrong impression.
There is much more to discover in Lost Cities than it might seem at first glance!


[#preparation]
== Preparation

Place the *5 A cards* (♠, ♥, ♣, ♦ & ★) in a line in the middle of the table, between the two players.
They mark the five spaces for the discard piles.

Put the ✿A and the 12 ✿ playing cards (9 Expedition cards & 3 Wager cards) back in the box.
They are not needed for the base game.

Shuffle the 60 playing cards.
Deal *eight cards* face down to each player.
These are your *hand*.
Place the remaining cards face down next to the line of A cards: they form the *draw pile*.

Have a pencil and paper ready to tally your scores.


== Object of the game

Your goal is to form expedition routes that earn you as many discovery points as possible, after subtracting the expedition costs.
You set up the expeditions by forming a separate column of cards for each suit.
The values within a column of cards must increase from card to card.
You can place Wager cards at the beginning of each column to multiply its value.
At the end of the round, the cards in each player's columns are scored.


== Sequence of play

The oldest player begins.
Then, the players alternate turns.
Each player only places cards on their side of the line of A cards.

During your turn, you must first play *one of the cards from your hand*.
Only then can you *draw one new card*.


=== 1. Play a card

Choose a card from your hand.
You have two options.


==== Option 1: place a card in one of your expedition columns

You can use this card to *start a new expedition* or to *expand an existing expedition*.
To do this, place the card face up on your side of the line, in front of the A card of the same suit.

You may only place additional cards at the end of a column.
Place the cards of a column so that they overlap, with the values of all the cards clearly visible.

IMPORTANT: Each new card you place in a column must have a *higher value* than the last card you placed in this column.

A *Wager card* may only be placed at the beginning of a column.
You may place several Wager cards on an expedition: 0, 1, 2 or 3 Wager cards before its Expedition cards.
However, as soon as you have placed an Expedition card on an expedition, you can no longer place any Wager card of its suit.


==== Option 2: discard a card

If you don't want to (or can't) place a card on an expedition, you must place a card from your hand face up on the A card of the same suit, whatever its value.
In this manner, five discard piles are formed during the game, one for each suit.
Place the cards on the piles so that only the top card is visible.


=== 2. Draw a card

Draw a new card into your hand, to get back to eight cards.
You have two options: take the top card of the draw pile *or* take the top card of one of the five discard piles (if there is a card there).

IMPORTANT: You are not allowed to draw the card you have just discarded.

Once you have drawn a card, your turn ends.


== End of the round and scoring

The round ends *as soon as* a player draws the last card of the draw pile.

NOTE: Near the end of the round, the players may agree to fan out the face-down draw pile a little and count the remaining cards, to estimate the number of turns left.

When the round ends, each player's expeditions are scored.
To do this, add up the values of all the cards in each expedition (each column of cards).
From each column's sum, subtract 20 points.
This is the expedition cost for that column.

IMPORTANT: If you have not placed *any card* of a suit, this suit incurs *no expedition cost*.

*After deducting the expedition cost*, if one, two or three Wager cards were placed at the beginning of a column, multiply the result by two, three or four, respectively.

Hence, a column can earn you positive or negative points.
If you haven't placed any card of a suit, you neither earn nor lose any points for this suit.

Furthermore, each column of *at least eight cards* earns its owner a bonus of 20 points.

IMPORTANT: This bonus is *not* multiplied by Wager cards!

Write down the points of each player.
Then start a new round as described in <<preparation>>.
This time, the player who has the most points goes first.
Add up your points after each round.

If after three rounds you have the highest overall score, you win!


== Variant: long game with six expeditions

The long game is played as described above, with the following changes.
During the preparation, place the ✿A at the end of the line to form a *sixth expedition space*.
Then shuffle all *72 playing cards*, including the 12 ✿ playing cards.

*Explanation of the ♥ column:* +
The Wager card doubles the value of the column.
Since no other card has been played, only the expedition cost is incurred, and it is doubled.

Also, only play Wager cards if you have enough cards for the expedition and there is still time to place them!
