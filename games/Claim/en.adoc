= Claim!
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 52 cards in 5 factions:
** 14 Goblins: 1-14 in ♠.
** 8 Knights: 1-8 in ♥.
** 10 Undead: 1-10 in ♣.
** 10 Dwarves: 1-10 in ♦.
** 10 Doppelgängers: 1-10 in ★.
****

The King is dead!
What happened?
Nobody really knows, but the King was found face down in a wine barrel this morning.
It could have been foul play, or the King's own thirst.
Regardless, the King is dead without any known heir, so it is up to the five factions of the realm to decide who will be the new king: will it be you or your opponent?
Do you have what it takes to win over the factions of the realm?


== Setup

Shuffle all the cards and place them face down in the middle of the table as a deck.
Deal 13 cards to each player.
Take your cards into your hand without showing them to your opponent.


== Game overview

The game is played in two distinct phases.
In phase one, each player gets a hand of cards that they use to recruit followers.
In phase two, they use the followers they gained in phase one to compete for the five factions of the realm.
At the end of the game, the player who has the majority of the followers of a faction wins that faction's vote.
The player who wins the vote of at least three factions wins the game!


== Phase one: recruit followers

This phase consists of thirteen tricks, one for each card in your hand.
The Leader of a trick is the player who won the previous trick (for the first trick, the youngest player).

Each trick is played in the three following steps.


=== Reveal a card

Flip the top card of the deck and place it face up between both players.
This is the card the players compete for.


=== Play cards

. The Leader plays any card from their hand.
+
NOTE: The Leader can play a card of any faction: it does not have to be of the faction of the card the players compete for.
. Then the other player plays a card from their hand.
+
[IMPORTANT]
====
If able, the other player must follow faction.
This means that if they have in hand a card of the faction just played, they must play one (exception: the Doppelgängers, ★).
In other words, you can only play a card of another faction if no card in your hand matches the faction of the card played by the Leader.
====


=== Collect cards

. The player who played the highest card of the faction played by the Leader wins the card in the middle.
If both cards have the same value, the Leader wins.
+
NOTE: If the other player played a card of another faction, the Leader automatically wins the trick, unless a faction power (Doppelgängers ★ or Knights ♥) applies.
. The winner places the card they won face down in their Follower deck.
The loser takes the top card of the deck and places it face down in their Follower deck.
They may look at this card, but must not show it to their opponent.
+
NOTE: The cards of your Follower deck will be your hand in phase two.
. If either player played an Undead (♣), the winner of the trick places it face up in their Score pile.
+
IMPORTANT: An Undead (♣) won from the middle of the table still goes to the winner's Follower deck.
. Discard all the other cards played.
+
IMPORTANT: Keep your Score pile face up and your Follower deck face down, so as not to mix them up.

Continue until the deck is empty and the players have no cards left in hand.
Then move on to phase two.


== Phase two: gather supporters

Each player takes into their hand the 13 cards of the Follower deck they gained in phase one.
The players now play another 13 tricks.
However, instead of competing for a card in the middle of the table, this time they compete for both cards played in each trick.

Each trick is played in the two following steps.


=== Play cards

. The Leader plays any card from their hand.
. Then the other player plays a card from their hand.
+
IMPORTANT: The obligation to follow faction still applies!


=== Collect cards

. Determine the winner of the trick as in phase one.
. The winner of the trick places both cards played face up in their Score pile, unless a faction power (Dwarves ♦) applies.

Once the players have played all their cards, score the factions.


== End of the game and scoring

Count how many cards of each faction you have in your Score pile.
The player who has the most cards of a faction wins that faction's vote.
If there is a tie, the player who has the highest card of that faction wins the vote.
The player who wins the vote of at least three factions wins the game!


== Faction powers

Each faction has a special power that affects play.

[%autowidth]
|===
| Faction | Suit | Power

| Goblins | ♠
| No special power.

| Knights | ♥
a| When played after a Goblin (♠), a Knight automatically beats it, whatever their values.

IMPORTANT: The player must still follow faction, if able.

| Undead | ♣
| In phase one, the Undead (♣) played are not discarded like the cards of the other factions.
They go to the Score pile of the trick's winner.

| Dwarves | ♦
| In phase two, the player who loses a trick takes all the Dwarves (♦) played in this trick and adds them to their Score pile.
The winner still takes the cards of the other factions played.

| Doppelgängers | ★
a| This faction is wild.
You may play a Doppelgänger (★) instead of the faction led, *even if you can follow faction*.
When played second, it counts as the faction of the first card, and so follows faction.

NOTE: If the Leader plays a Doppelgänger (★), the other player must still follow faction by playing a Doppelgänger (★), if able.

IMPORTANT: A Doppelgänger (★) does not take the special power of the faction it follows: played after an Undead (♣) in phase one, it is not taken by the winner, and played after a Dwarf (♦) in phase two, it is not taken by the loser.
|===
