= Hats
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 30 hat cards: 1-6 in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦ & ★.
* 12 more hat cards with 3 or 4 players: 1-6 in ✿ & ⎈.
* The Ø card, to mark the start of the tea line.
* Paper and a pencil to keep the score.
****

You are invited to the Mad Hatter’s garden at 5 pm.
The table is ready, the cookies taste like buttery heaven, and the tea is strong and spicy. +
— More sugar? +
The Mad Hatter gives you a strange look. +
— Yes, pl… +
— Time’s up! +
The Mad Hatter cuts you off.
Sugar, cookies and millions of hats fly everywhere.
Tea spills all over the tablecloth, and a huge smile lights up the Mad Hatter’s face. +
— It’s time to play a game!

In Hats, two to four players compete to collect the most outstanding hats, by exchanging cards from their hand with cards of the tea line.
Each exchange changes how each hat scores.
Naturally, at the end of the game, the player with the highest score is declared the maddest.
Get ready for some madness!

NOTE: The suit of a card is its hat type.


== Setup

. Shuffle the hat cards to form the draw deck, and place it face down at the side of the table.
With 2 players, use only the ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦ & ★ cards.
With 3 or 4 players, use all the hat cards.
. Place the Ø card on the table, then draw six hat cards from the draw deck and place them face up in a line on the table: this is the tea line.
*With 2 players, draw only five cards.*
The cards must form a line, with the Ø card at one end.
The hat card closest to the Ø card is at position 1 of the tea line, and the card furthest from it is at position 6 (or position 5 with 2 players).
. The player who most recently ate a cookie starts the game.
. Deal nine hat cards to each player.
Keep your cards secret!


== Gameplay

On your turn, perform one of the following two actions.


=== Actions

==== 1. Exchange hats

Play a card from your hand face up, and exchange it with a card of the tea line, if:

* both cards are of the same type (the same suit),
* *or* the number on the card you play is higher than the number on the card you take.

Your card takes the place of the card you take in the tea line.
Add the card you take to your collection, by placing it face up in front of you.

IMPORTANT: You can only exchange cards with the tea line.
Cards cannot be exchanged between players.


==== 2. Create a black hat

Play a card face down in front of you to add it to your collection.
This card is a black hat.
At the end of the game, each black hat in your collection is worth one point.


==== Optional action

At any time during your turn, you may discard a card of your choice and draw a new one from the draw deck.
Discarded cards go face down near the draw deck.
If the draw deck runs out, shuffle the discard pile to form a new draw deck.


=== Playing with 4 players

Follow the normal rules, with the following change: you play in teams of two.
Sit so that each player’s partner is sitting across from them, with an opponent on each side.

*New optional action: instead of discarding a card and drawing a new one, at any time during your turn, you may exchange a card face down with your partner.*

You may freely talk about what to exchange, but you cannot name the type (the suit) or the number of the cards.


== Scoring

The game ends after all players have played eight cards.
Each player reveals the last card in their hand: its type is their “favorite hat”.
Then, if there are two or more hat cards of the same type in the tea line, keep face up the card of that type with the lowest position in the tea line, and turn all the other cards of that type face down.

Calculate the scores in each of the three categories.


=== Hat collection

You earn points for the hat cards of your collection, based on the position of the matching type in the tea line.
For example, if a ♥ card is at position 3 of the tea line, each ♥ card in your collection is worth three points.
Each black hat in front of you is worth one point.
Face-down cards of the tea line do not award points.
The hat cards of your collection without a matching face-up card in the tea line do not award points.

=== Favorite hat

Add up the values of the cards in your collection that match the type of your favorite hat, then subtract the value of the last card in your hand.
The last card in your hand is not part of your collection.
This score can be negative.

.Favorite hat scoring
====
With the same collection as in the previous example, your favorite hat card is [⎈2].

You score stem:[6 + 5 - 2 = 9] points.
====


=== Last cookie

There is only one cookie.
To get it, have the most different types of hat cards in your collection.
Black hats count as a type.
In case of a tie, the tied player with the lowest hat card in their collection gets the cookie.
If there is still a tie, compare the second lowest card, then the third lowest, and so on until the tie is broken.
*The cookie is worth five points.*

The player with the most points wins the game.
In case of a tie, the player with the most black hats in their collection wins.
If there is still a tie, the player who has the cookie wins.


=== Scoring with 4 players

Calculate the score of each player.
Then add up the scores of both members of a team to get the team’s score.
