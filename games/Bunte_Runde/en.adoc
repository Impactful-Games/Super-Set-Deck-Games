= Bunte Runde
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 36 cards: 1-6 in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿ & ★.
** _Short game_: only 25 of them, 1-5 in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦ & ✿.
* 1 pawn: the Monkey.
* Paper and a pencil to keep the score.
****

Cards of 6 suits and 6 values are laid out in a circle, and the players take them one by one.
When a suit or a value has been completely taken, every player who has one or more cards of it scores points.
By skillfully moving the pawn, you try to take the cards that bring the most points.

Younger players may prefer the short game, with 25 cards of 5 suits and 5 values.
The rules are the same, but the game is more concise and shorter.

TIP: Instead of the Monkey, you can use any small object as a pawn: a key, a bottle cap…


== Setup

. Choose to play the normal game, with the 36 cards, or the short game, with the 25 cards 1-5 in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦ & ✿.
The other cards are not needed.
. Shuffle the cards and lay them out face up in a circle.
. Place the pawn on any card of the circle.
. Take a sheet of paper and a pencil to write down the scores.


== Playing the game

The youngest player begins.
You take turns in clockwise order.

On your turn, move the pawn one, two or three cards forward, clockwise along the circle.
**Skip the gaps**: count only the cards still in the circle.
Then take the card the pawn has landed on and place it face up in front of you, so that it is visible to all players.
The pawn stays in the gap left by that card.


[#scoring]
== Scoring

=== Basic scoring

When you take the last card of a suit (for example the last ♦ card) *or* the last card of a value (for example the last 3), the game is interrupted and a scoring takes place.

Every player who has cards of this suit or value in front of them scores 1 point for each of these cards.
Each scoring thus awards 6 points in all (5 in the short game).


=== Double scoring

If the card you take is the last one of its suit *and* the last one of its value, there is a double scoring: 6 (or 5) points are awarded for its suit, and 6 (or 5) for its value.


== End of the game

The game ends as soon as the total of the points scored by all the players reaches 36 (25 in the short game) or more.
The player with the most points wins.
