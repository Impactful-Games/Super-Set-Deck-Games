= Jalape-No!
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 108 cards: 0-15, Q & K in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿ & ★ (the 0s are the Jalapeno cards).
* Paper and a pencil to keep the score.
****

Nothing else compares to the delectability of these dishes, but the intensity of even a single Jalapeno can throw the whole dish out of balance.
Build up your collection of food options, but whatever you do, stay away from those Jalapeno cards!
If you do catch a Jalapeno card, try to get rid of it by the end of the round.
The chef who collects the fewest penalty points after five rounds is the winner!


== Game setup

Depending on the number of players, use the following cards:

[%autowidth]
|===
| Players | Suits | Values

| 3 | ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦ | 0-8
| 4 | ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦ | 0-11
| 5 | ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ★ | 0-11
| 6 | ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿, ★ | 0-11
| 7 | ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿, ★ | 0-13
| 8 | ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿, ★ | 0-15
| 9 | ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿, ★ | 0-15, Q, K
|===

NOTE: With 9 players, the Q cards have a value of 16, and the K cards a value of 17.

Shuffle the cards and deal 12 cards to each player.

*The 0 cards are the Jalapeno cards.*
If you were dealt any Jalapeno cards, immediately place them face up on the table in front of you.
Jalapeno cards score penalty points if you still have them at the end of the round.
Only Jalapeno cards are ever kept face up on the table this way.

Choose a starting player by any method you wish (for example, the person who most recently ate spicy food).


== How to play

=== Playing a card

The starting player begins by choosing one card from their hand or one of their Jalapeno cards, and plays it face up in the center of the table.

The card played by the starting player sets the led suit, which all the other players must play, provided they have at least one card of that suit *in their hand*.
In clockwise order, the other players play one card from their hand or from their Jalapenos.

On your turn, you have to follow suit if you can, with one exception: *you never have to play a Jalapeno card if you don't want to*.

.Playing a card
====
The starting player chooses to lead a ♥ card.
On your turn, you must also play the led suit if possible.
These are the possibilities:

* You have ♥ cards (and maybe other suits) in hand, and a ♥ Jalapeno (and maybe others) on the table.
You can play any ♥ card from your hand, or your ♥ Jalapeno.
* You have ♥ cards (and maybe other suits) in hand, and no ♥ Jalapeno on the table (but you may have other Jalapenos).
You must play a ♥ card from your hand.
* You have no ♥ cards in hand, and a ♥ Jalapeno (and maybe others) on the table.
You can play any card from your hand, or your ♥ Jalapeno, but no other Jalapeno.
* You have no ♥ cards in hand, and no ♥ Jalapeno on the table (but you may have other Jalapenos).
You can play any card from your hand or from your Jalapenos.
====


=== Taking tricks

One turn of each player choosing and playing a card is called a trick.
The player who played the highest card of the led suit wins the trick.
Cards that are not of the led suit cannot win the trick, no matter how high their value.

If you win the trick, you take its cards.
If any Jalapeno cards are included, immediately add them to your collection of Jalapenos, face up on the table: you can play them in future tricks.
Place the other cards you won face down in a pile.
They are not used again until the end of the round.

As the winner of the trick, you become the new starting player.

Players continue playing tricks until at least one player has no cards left *in their hand*.
The round ends after that trick, and each player places the cards remaining in their hand in their own face-down pile.


=== Scoring

Each Jalapeno card is worth -2 points.
Each card in your pile of the same suit as one of your Jalapenos is worth -1 point.

All the other cards in your face-down pile score 0 points.
If you don't have a Jalapeno card, you don't get any penalty points.

At the end of a round, the player with the most penalty points becomes the starting player of the new round.
After 5 rounds, the player with the fewest penalty points wins.
