= Nyet!
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 60 playing cards: 1-12 & 3 K in ♠, ♥, ♣ & ♦ (without 10-12 with 3 players).
* 19 Nyet! cards:
** 5 discard cards: 0-2, ► & K in ⎈.
** 4 trump cards: A in ♠, ♥, ♣ & ♦.
** 5 supertrump cards: S in ♠, ♥, ♣ & ♦, and the Ø card.
** 5 trick value cards: 1-4 & -‽ in ★.
* 1 Bonus card: ★×2.
* Character cards, 2 per player: 2 Butterflies & 2 Wolves, plus 2 Phoenixes with 3 players or more, 2 Snakes with 4 players or more, 2 Dragons with 5 players or more, and 2 Monkeys with 6 players.
* Paper and a pencil to keep the score.

TIP: Instead of using the Nyet! cards, you can play with a link:Nyet-Board.pdf[board] and tokens to cover the options on it.
****

Players are split into 2 teams that each try to score points by taking tricks.

Each trick is influenced by the players' decisions at the start of the round.
For example, players choose the trump suit and the value of the tricks.

In each round, players remove Nyet! cards of their choice, deciding what will NOT be allowed in the round.
For example, if a player removes the ♥A trump card, ♥ will not be the trump suit for the round.


== Setup

* Choose a Character and lay one of its two cards face up in front of you.

* Place the Nyet! cards in the center of the table in 5 lines:
** *First player*: one Character card per player (the second card of the Character each player chose).
** *Discard*: ⎈0, ⎈1, ⎈2, ⎈► & ⎈K.
** *Trump*: ♠A, ♥A, ♣A & ♦A.
** *Supertrump*: ♠S, ♥S, ♣S, ♦S & the Ø card.
** *Points*: ★1, ★2, ★3, ★4 & ★-‽.

* With 3 players, remove 12 playing cards (see <<setup-specs>>).
With 2, 4, 5 or 6 players, use all 60 playing cards.

* Choose one player to take paper and a pencil and to be in charge of keeping the score.
They write down the points of the players during the game.

* Choose a dealer at random.
The dealer shuffles the playing cards and deals them one by one to the players, face down.


== Game play

The game is played over several rounds (depending on the number of players).
Each round is divided into 2 phases: the Nyet! phase and the trick phase.
During the Nyet! phase, the players determine the terms of the trick phase that follows.


=== Playing a round

To begin a round, the dealer shuffles the playing cards and deals them to the players.
Then go to the Nyet! phase.


=== The Nyet! phase

Players look at their cards and try to influence the terms of the upcoming trick phase in their favour by removing Nyet! cards.

The dealer always begins by removing one Nyet! card of their choice from the table.
For example, if you only have a few ♠ cards, you might remove the ♠A Nyet! card from the table, deciding that ♠ is not the trump suit for this round.

Once the dealer has removed a Nyet! card, the next player clockwise does the same, and so on.
The last Nyet! card of a line may never be removed.
As soon as only one Nyet! card is left in each line (5 cards in all), the Nyet! phase ends.


=== The Nyet! cards

The card left in each line on the table sets the terms of the current round.


==== Line 1: first player [Character cards]

The player whose Character card is left on the table is the first player.
The first player chooses their team.
The other players become the opposing team.
The first player chooses who receives the Bonus card, and plays the first card of the trick phase.


==== Line 2: discard [⎈ cards]

For this round, each player must discard the number of cards shown, face down.
These cards are not used during the round and do not count for points.

If the ⎈K card is left on the table, each player must discard one card, which must *not* be a K.

If the ⎈► card is left on the table, each player must instead give one card of their choice to their left neighbour.

If the ⎈0 card is left on the table, nobody discards any cards.


==== Line 3: trump [A cards]

The trump suit for the round is the suit of the A card left on the table.
The trump suit can be ♠, ♥, ♣ or ♦.


==== Line 4: supertrump [S cards]

The three K cards of the suit of the S card left on the table are the supertrumps.
The supertrumps can be the K cards of ♠, ♥, ♣ or ♦, or there can be no supertrump at all in the round (the Ø card).
If you chose a supertrump in the Nyet! phase, it turns double agent!
Whatever its original suit, during the trick phase, the supertrump counts as a card of the trump suit.


==== Line 5: points (trick/loot) [★ cards]

Card values do not matter for scoring.
Each trick taken and each K card captured from the opposing team (loot) earns the number of points of the ★ card left on the table.
If the ★-‽ card is left on the table, each trick and each loot of the round is worth -2 points (see <<loot>>).


=== The trick phase

Before the trick phase actually begins:

* The first player sets the teams by choosing their teammates.
* Each player discards the number of cards shown (or gives 1 card to their left neighbour, if required).

Then the first player begins the trick phase by playing a card of their choice face up on the table.

The other players play clockwise, each playing one card face up in turn.
They must follow these rules:

* You must follow the suit of the first card played, if you can.
* If you cannot follow, you may play any card from your hand (including a trump or a supertrump).
* If the first card is a trump, you must play a trump or a supertrump, if you can.
* If the first card is a supertrump, you must play a trump or a supertrump, if you can.

Once each player has played a card, check who takes the trick (a trick being one card played by each player).

.5-player game
====
The Wolf card is left on line 1.
So the Wolf player is the first player, and forms a team of 3 players with the Butterfly and Dragon players.
They give the Bonus card to a player of the opposing team (the Phoenix or the Snake player). +
The ⎈0 card is left on the table, so no cards are discarded. +
The ♣A card shows that the trump suit is ♣. +
The ♠S card shows that the ♠ K cards are the supertrumps. +
Finally, the ★2 card shows that in this round, each trick and each loot is worth 2 points (see <<loot>>).
====


=== Who takes the trick?

* The highest card of the led suit takes the trick, unless a trump or a supertrump has been played.
+
CAUTION: The K cards that are not supertrumps have a value of 0.
+
If several cards of the same value and the same suit are played, the last one played takes the trick (this also applies to trumps and supertrumps).
* If one or more trumps are played without any supertrump, the highest trump takes the trick.
* If a supertrump is played, it takes the trick.

The player who took the trick picks up its cards and stacks them face down in front of them, keeping each trick won separate.

They begin the next trick by playing a card of their choice.

Once all the tricks have been played (and no player has cards left in hand), score the points.

Each team counts the number of tricks won and of loot cards captured (see below).
This total is multiplied by the points of the round, shown by the ★ card left on line 5.
The scorekeeper writes the results down.


[#loot]
== The loot

The K cards become loot when they are captured from the opposing team.
This also applies to supertrumps.
When a player wins a trick that contains a K card played by an opponent, they claim it as loot.
Each captured K card is placed face up next to the pile of tricks won in this round.
It is possible to win up to 3 loot cards in one trick.

.Trick
====
The first player (on a 3-player team) plays ♦8. +
Their teammate plays ♦K, then a player of the opposing team plays ♦3. +
The second player of the opposing team has no ♦ cards and chooses to play ♣K (♣ is the trump suit). +
To finish, the last player of the 3-player team has no ♦ cards either, and plays ♣6. +
This last player wins the trick and takes the 5 cards: the ♣K face up in front of them, and the other 4 cards in a face-down pile.
The ♦K is not loot, since one of their teammates played it.
====


== New round

After scoring, place all the Nyet! cards back on the table.
The player to the left of the last dealer becomes the new dealer.
They shuffle all the playing cards and deal them again.
A new round begins.


== End of the game

The game ends after a set number of rounds.

[%autowidth]
|===
h| Players | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6
h| Rounds | 8 | 9 | 8 | 10 | 12
|===

*Each player deals the same number of times.*

At the end of the game, the player with the most points wins.
In case of a tie, there are several winners.

[TIP]
.Variant
====
You may also play with a score target set at the start of the game.
For instance, you may decide that the game ends as soon as a player reaches 100 points.
====


[#setup-specs]
== Setup details

=== Bonus card ★×2

Use this card only with 3 or 5 players.

The player or the team with fewer players receives the Bonus card.
With 5 players, the first player decides which of the 2 players of that team receives it (they can give it to themselves).
This card doubles the points its owner scores in the round.


=== 6 players

Use all 60 cards.
Deal 10 cards to each player.
Teams are 3 vs. 3, and the first player chooses their teammates.
The game ends after 12 rounds.


=== 5 players

Use all 60 cards.
Deal 12 cards to each player.
Teams are 3 vs. 2.
The first player chooses whether they play on the team of 2 or of 3 players.
They also choose who plays on which team.
They give the Bonus card to one player of the 2-player team (possibly themselves).
This player doubles the points they score from tricks and loot in this round.
The game ends after 10 rounds.


=== 4 players

Use all 60 cards.
Deal 15 cards to each player.
Teams are 2 vs. 2, and the first player chooses their teammate.
The game ends after 8 rounds.


=== 3 players

Remove the 10, 11 and 12 of each suit, leaving 48 cards in the deck, and deal 16 cards to each player.
Teams are 1 vs. 2.
The first player chooses whether they play alone or with a partner.
The solo player receives the Bonus card: all their points are doubled.
The game ends after 9 rounds.


=== 2 players

Use all 60 cards.
Deal 15 cards to each player.
The 30 cards left are not used in this round.
Set them aside face down, and shuffle them back into the deck for the next round.
The game ends after 8 rounds.
