= Bids
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 56 bid cards: 1-14 in ♠, ♥, ♣ & ♦, depending on the number of players.
* 16 point cards: 1-14, ×2 & -‽ in ★, depending on the number of players.
* 2 Monkeys, with 5 or 6 players.
* 4 special cards (_Special cards variant_): Wolf, Phoenix, Snake & Butterfly.
* Paper and a pencil to keep the score.
****

Each player is dealt ten bid cards.
In every round, you bid in ten turns for the ★ point cards in the middle.
Each ★ point card is worth as many points as the number written on it.
The suits of the bid cards do not matter in the basic game.
The player with the most points at the end of the game wins.

| 5 players
a|
* 1-12 in ♠, ♥, ♣ & ♦
* 1-12, ×2 & -‽ in ★

| 6 players
a|
* 1-14 in ♠, ♥, ♣ & ♦
* 1-14, ×2 & -‽ in ★
* 2 Monkeys

Set the ★ point cards apart, and shuffle the bid cards into one deck.
With 3 to 5 players, take one random bid card out of the deck and remove it from play.
With 6 players, all the bid cards are used.
With 5 or 6 players, shuffle one Monkey into the deck of bid cards, and set the second one aside.
Add one random ★ point card to the deck of bid cards (two with 5 players, three with 6 players), and deal ten cards from the deck to each player.
Put the rest of the ★ point cards face down in the middle of the table.


[#playing-the-game]
== Playing the game

At the start of each turn, flip over the top point card.
This is the card you bid for.

At every turn, all players must bid exactly one of their ten cards, one by one in clockwise order.

In the first turn of the first round, the player with the longest first name (last name as a tiebreaker) starts bidding.
The player with the highest bid wins the turn, takes the ★ point card from the middle and places it in front of them.
The bid cards played are discarded and have no further use in this round.

In case of a tie between the highest bids, the point cards of that turn are "burned" and nobody receives any of them.
Place the burned point cards at the bottom of the point card pile.

In the next turn, the player who placed the highest bid starts bidding or, in case of a tie, the last player who burned (the Burner is the Turner).


=== ★ cards in hand

A player can have a ★ point card in their hand, received when the cards were dealt.
They must bid with this card like any other card, but the value of this ★ card is added to the value of the turn.

.★ bid
====
You are bidding for a ★5 and one of the players bids a ★8: the winner of the turn receives 8 + 5 points.
If the ★8 is the highest bid (and there is no tie), its player wins the 8 + 5 points.
If the bid of another player is higher, that player takes both cards and receives 8 + 5 points.
====


=== End of a round

A round ends after ten turns.
This means that not all the point cards in the middle are turned over.
Add the points of the round to those of the previous rounds, and start the next round.

In the next round, the player with the highest total score starts bidding in the first turn.
In case of a tie, the score of the last round is the tiebreaker.
If the tie persists, the player who won the highest card in the last round starts.

The game ends after six rounds, unless all players agree on a different number.


=== The lucky loser

All players who do not have any ★ card in front of them at the end of a round receive lucky loser points.
These points depend on the number of players and on how many times they have received lucky loser points, this time included.

.4 players
====
A player wins 9 points in round 1.
In the next three rounds, they end without any ★ card.
In the fifth round, they receive 13 points, and in the last round they end without any ★ card in front of them.

After six rounds, they have stem:[9 + (1 xx 8) + (2 xx 8) + (3 xx 8) + 13 + (4 xx 8) = 102] points.
====


== Suit bids

In this variant, the highest card of the suit, or suits, with the most cards played in the turn wins.

.Suit bids
====
In a turn, the following cards are played: ♥4, ♠5, ♣9 and ♠7.
In suit bids, the ♠7 wins, because there are two ♠ cards and only one ♥ and one ♣.
====

Always add 1 to the count of ★ cards, because the ★ card in the middle counts.
In the special case where ★ is the suit that appears the most and the highest ★ card on the table is the one in the middle, the turn is "burned" and the player who started the previous turn starts again.


== Special cards

With 5 or 6 players, the Monkeys are always used, and the ×2 and -‽ cards are always part of the ★ point cards.
Optionally, you can add the Wolf, the Phoenix, the Snake and the Butterfly to the pile of ★ point cards.
If a special card other than the -‽ card is in the hand of a player, they play it like any other card, but they never win the turn in which they play it.


=== The Monkey

The Monkey is always shuffled into the deck of bid cards, and therefore always ends up in the hand of a player.
This player plays the Monkey like any other card, and works together with the winner of the turn in which they played it.
At the end of the round, the points of both players are added up and divided by two (rounding up an odd number).
In case of a tie between the highest bids, there is no alliance.
Only one Monkey is used in the game: place the second Monkey next to the player who played the first one, to mark the alliance.

When a player is in an alliance but does not have any ★ card at the end of the round, they do not receive the lucky loser points of this round, but their lucky loser count still increases by 1.

.5 players
====
In round 1, a player ends without any ★ card: they receive 8 points.

In round 2, they are in an alliance with another player, but they do not have any ★ card at the end of the round.
They receive half of the alliance points, but not the 16 points of a lucky loser round.

In round 3, they end again without ★ cards: they now receive 24 points.
====


=== The -‽ card

The player who wins the turn in which this card appears receives -5 points.

If, at the end of the round, a player only has this card, their result for the round is -5 points, and they do not receive the lucky loser points.

If this card is in the hand of a player, they bid -5 when they play it, and the -‽ card goes to the winner of the turn.

If the highest bid is a tie, nobody receives this card.


=== The ×2 card

For the player who wins this card, all the points received in the previous turns of the round are doubled.
This card has no effect on the points received afterwards.
The point cards played in the same turn as the ×2 card are doubled too.

If this card is in the hand of a player, they play it, and the ×2 card goes to the winner of the turn.
You can never win this card if it was in your hand.

If the highest bid is a tie, nobody receives this card.


=== The Wolf

The player who wins the turn in which this card appears must steal one ★ point card from the player who made the lowest bid of that turn.

.Wolf
====
The bids are 10, 8, 6 and 5: the player who bid 10 must steal one card from the player who bid 5.
They choose one of the cards that the player who bid 5 won earlier in the round.
====

If the player with the lowest bid has not won any card in this round, the winner of the turn steals from the lowest bidder who has already won a card.
If the lowest bid is a tie, the winner of the turn chooses whom to steal from.

If the highest bid is a tie, nobody receives this card.


=== The Phoenix

When this card appears, there is no bidding for it.
Shuffle the pile of remaining point cards again, and discard the Phoenix.

This means that all the point cards that were burned (see <<playing-the-game>>) can appear again.
Turn over the next point card, and the bidding continues.

If this card is in the hand of a player, shuffle the pile of ★ cards at the end of the turn, before turning over the next ★ card.
If, in this case, the highest bid is a tie, do not shuffle the pile.


=== The Snake

When this card appears, there is no bidding for it.
From now on, the lowest bidder wins every turn until the end of the round.
Put the Snake aside, and turn over the next point card.

If this card is in the hand of a player, the "lowest bidder wins" rule applies from the next turn on, as long as the highest bid is not a tie.
If the highest bid is a tie, the rule does not apply in this round.


=== The Butterfly

When this card appears, you bid for the next point card of the pile without knowing what it is.

If this card is in the hand of a player, the winner receives the next point card, and the point card you were bidding for goes to the bottom of the pile of point cards.

If the highest bid is a tie, nobody receives any card.
