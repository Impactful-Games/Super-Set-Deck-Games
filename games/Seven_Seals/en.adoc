= Seven Seals
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 45 numbered cards: 1-9 in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦ & ★.
* 15 more numbered cards with 4 or 5 players: 10-12 in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦ & ★.
* 15 more numbered cards with 5 players: 13-15 in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦ & ★.
* 27 seals:
** 17 suit seals:
*** 12 seals for the ♠, ♥, ♣ & ♦ suits: the 3 K in ♠, ♥, ♣ & ♦.
*** 5 seals for the trump suit: the 3 K, the C & the S in ★.
+
NOTE: ★C and ★S are ★ seals, just like ★K: the deck only has 3 ★K.
** 10 magic seals: 6 Wolves & 4 Butterflies.
* The black magician: ⎈A.
* Paper and a pencil to keep the score.
****

Who will have the greatest power of divination?
Back at the magicians’ academy, the apprentices of magic face a new challenge.
Each budding magician has to predict how many tricks they will win, and in which suits.
To record their prediction, they take a matching number of suit seals, which they have to get rid of during the game.
Besides the suit seals, there are also magic seals: the Wolves and the Butterflies.
And as if predicting were not hard enough, there is also an unpredictable black magician, whose only goal is to spoil the other apprentices’ plans.
The apprentice with the fewest penalty points after several rounds wins the game, and becomes Master of the Seven Seals.


== Setup

Depending on the number of apprentices, shuffle these cards and deal them evenly:

* Three apprentices: the cards 1-9 of each suit.
* Four apprentices: the cards 1-12 of each suit.
* Five apprentices: all 75 numbered cards.

The remaining cards, if any, go back in the box for the whole game.
Each apprentice gets a hand of 15 cards.

Place the black magician and the 27 seals in the center of the table.


== Prediction

The apprentice to the left of the dealer makes the first prediction.
Then each of the other apprentices makes one, clockwise.
On your turn, you must:

* either predict how many tricks you will win in each suit, and take the matching number of seals of these suits,
* or take the black magician (⎈A).

*Only one apprentice* can take *the black magician*.
Once the black magician is gone, the other apprentices can only predict their tricks.
No one has to take the black magician.
You may also take no seal at all, and thus predict that you will win no trick.


=== The black magician and the Wolf seals

Taking the black magician is advisable only if you have very few or no cards of a suit.
The apprentice who takes it gets no seal this round.
Their job is to make the other apprentices take penalty seals by spoiling their plans, either by winning tricks themselves or by discarding cards.
Read more in <<battle-for-tricks>> and <<penalty-points>>.

.Predictions
====
* Frank starts predicting their tricks.
They take one ★ seal, two ♠ seals and one ♣ seal from the center of the table.
They plan to win one ★ trick, two ♠ tricks and one ♣ trick.
* Sabine also takes seals from the center of the table: two ♥ seals, one ♠ seal and three ★ seals.
* Katrin does not predict any trick.
Instead, they take the black magician (⎈A) and place it in front of them.
Now they have to prevent their opponents from getting rid of their seals, or even make them take Wolf seals.
====


=== The Butterfly seals

The Butterfly seals only come into play if the seals of a suit have run out.
If, for example, an apprentice wants to take a ♦ seal, but there are no more ♦ seals in the center of the table, they have to take a ♦ seal from any other apprentice.
That apprentice immediately gets a Butterfly seal from the center of the table, as a compensation.
The Butterfly seals act as jokers during the round.
See also <<battle-for-tricks>> and <<penalty-points>>.

.Butterfly
====
* It is Peter’s turn.
They want to win two ♦ tricks, one ♣ trick and one ♠ trick.
First, they take two ♦ seals and one ♣ seal from the center of the table.
As there are no more ♠ seals there, they take the ♠ seal they need from Sabine.
Sabine gets a Butterfly seal instead.
====


[#battle-for-tricks]
== Battle for tricks

The apprentice to the dealer’s left starts the first trick: they play any card from their hand face up in the middle of the table.
Then, clockwise, each other apprentice plays a card face up on it.
If you have a card of the suit led, you must play one.
If you do not have any card of the suit led, you can either discard a card of any other suit, or win the trick by playing a ★, as ★ is always trump.


=== Who wins the trick, and so the cards in the middle of the table?

The apprentice who played the highest ★ (trump) wins the trick.
If there is no ★ in the trick, the apprentice who played the highest card of the suit led wins it.
The winner takes all the cards of the trick and places them face down in front of them.

Then they must return one of their seals of the suit of the trick to the center of the table.
If they have no seal of this suit, they must take a Wolf seal from the center of the table.
At the end of the round, each Wolf seal is worth three penalty points.

The winner of the trick then leads the next trick with any card.

IMPORTANT: If the first card of a trick is a ★, its winner must return a ★ seal.
If another suit was led, but the trick was won with a ★, its winner may return either a ★ seal or a seal of the suit led.

Butterfly seals are jokers: they can be returned for any trick won.
The apprentice who took the black magician (⎈A) has no seals to return: if they win a trick, they simply place it aside face down, and they do not receive any penalty seal.
They then lead the next trick with any card.

.Three different tricks
====
* Frank goes first and plays ♥2 (the next apprentices must play a ♥ card if they can).
Sabine plays ♥11.
Katrin follows suit with ♥7.
Peter does not have any ♥ card, so they play their ♣12 to get rid of it.
Sabine wins the trick with ♥11, so they return one of their ♥ seals to the center of the table.
* Sabine leads the next trick and plays ♦4.
Katrin follows suit with ♦2, Peter plays ♦9, and Frank has to play their only ♦ card, ♦10.
Frank wins the trick, but they do not have a ♦ seal.
They have to take a Wolf penalty seal from the center of the table.
* Frank leads the next trick and plays ♥8.
Sabine does not have any ♥ card left, so they trump with ★3.
Katrin plays ♥1.
Peter still has no ♥ card, so they discard their ♣8.
Sabine wins the trick, because they played a ★.
They can return either a ★ seal or a ♥ seal.
====


[#penalty-points]
== Penalty points

The round is over as soon as the apprentices have played all their cards, and they count their penalty points:

* An apprentice with no seals left gets 0 penalty points.
* Each suit seal still in front of an apprentice is worth two penalty points.
* Each Wolf seal is worth three penalty points.
* Each Butterfly seal is worth four penalty points.
* The apprentice who took the black magician (⎈A) gets four penalty points at most.
These four points are reduced by one point for each Wolf seal the other apprentices had to take.
For example, if the other apprentices had to take three Wolf seals altogether, the black magician gets only one penalty point (stem:[4 - 3 = 1]).
If the black magician has spoiled the other apprentices’ game so well that they had to take four Wolf seals or more, the black magician gets no penalty point.

.Penalty points
====
* Frank gets three penalty points for their Wolf seal.
* Sabine gets four penalty points for their Butterfly seal.
* Katrin, the black magician, gets two penalty points, because the other apprentices took two Wolf seals in total (stem:[4 - 2 = 2]).
* Peter gets five penalty points: two for their ♦ seal and three for their Wolf seal.
====

Write down the penalty points and add them to the previous ones, then return all the seals and the black magician to the center of the table.
The next player clockwise becomes the dealer, shuffles the cards and deals 15 cards to each apprentice, and the apprentice to their left starts the new round.


== End of the game

Play as many rounds as there are apprentices, except with three apprentices: then, play six rounds.

The apprentice with the fewest penalty points at the end of the game wins.


== Hints and strategies

* An apprentice who wins a trick has to return a seal immediately.
Returning it later is not allowed.
* There are only six Wolf seals per round.
The apprentice who should take a seventh Wolf seal is lucky: they do not take one!
* When predicting tricks, keep in mind that trumps (★) can win tricks of any suit, and that you can then return either a ★ seal or a seal of the suit led.
* Taking the black magician can be interesting if you have no or very few cards of one or more suits.
Each time you cannot follow suit, you can spoil the other apprentices’ plans, either by trumping to win the trick, or by discarding a high card of another suit.
* With three apprentices, the black magician is very attractive, as it is very lucrative.
To make it less attractive, you can agree before the game that it is worth five penalty points instead of four.
* For a longer game, you can agree to play eight rounds with four apprentices, or ten rounds with five.
In this case, the designer recommends raising the black magician’s penalty from four to five points after the first half of the rounds.
