= Odin
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 54 cards: 1-9 in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿ & ★.
* Paper and a pencil to keep the score.
****

Be the first to get rid of all the cards in your hand, and have the fewest points at the end of the game.


== Setup

. Shuffle all the cards.
. Deal 9 cards face down to each player: this is your hand.
Look at your hand, but don't let anyone else see it!

Choose a first player at random.
You then take turns in clockwise order.


== How to play

The game is played over several hands.
Each hand is divided into rounds.

NOTE: A round is made of several turns, and it ends when all players but one have passed.

At the start of a round, the first player plays 1 of their cards face up in the middle of the table.

Then, on your turn, you either:

1. play 1 or more cards;
2. or pass.


=== 1. Play 1 or more cards

The value of the cards you play must be greater than the value of the cards already in the middle of the table.

.Higher value
====
If the value in the middle of the table is 3, you must play a value of 4 or more.
====

You can play either the same number of cards as there are in the middle of the table, or 1 card more.

.More cards
====
If there is a set of 2 cards in the middle of the table, you can play a set of 2 or 3 cards, but not a set of 4 cards, nor 1 card on its own.
====

If you want to play several cards, they must all have the same number or the same suit.

When you play several cards, their value is the highest number you can make by using the number of each card as a digit.

.Value of a set
====
If you play ♠2 and ♠8, the value of the set is 82, not 28.

If you play ♥2, ♥4 and ♥9, the value of the set is 942.
====

Once you have played 1 or more cards, you must pick up 1 of the cards that were in the middle of the table before you played.
You cannot pick up a card you have just played.
So:

* If there is only 1 card, you must pick it up.
* If there is a set of cards, choose the card you want to keep and discard the others.


=== 2. Pass

If you pass, you do not play any cards, and the player on your left takes their turn.

NOTE: Even if you pass, you can play as normal on your next turn.

If all players but 1 pass, the round ends: discard the cards left in the middle of the table.
The last player who played cards starts the new round.
Everyone keeps the cards in their hand.


== End of a hand

A hand can end in 2 ways:

. If you start a new round and all the cards in your hand have the same number or the same suit, you may play them all.
The hand is then over.
If you cannot, play 1 card as normal.
. If, at any time, you play 1 or more cards and your hand is then empty, do not pick up a card from the middle of the table.
The hand is then over.

At the end of a hand, you score 1 point for each card left in your hand.
Note your points on paper.

To start a new hand, collect all the cards, shuffle them and deal 9 cards to each player.
The player to the left of the previous hand's first player starts the new hand by playing 1 card.


== End of the game

For your first game, we recommend playing to 15 points: once a player has 15 points or more, the game ends, and the player with the fewest points wins.
In case of a tie, the tied players share the victory.

After your first game, you can choose the number of points you play to: add 5 points for a longer game (20 points), or remove 5 points for a shorter one (10 points).
For a very short game, just play 1 hand!
