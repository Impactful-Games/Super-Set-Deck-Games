= Apocalypse

:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 27 Horseman cards: 1-4 & 6-10 in ♠, ♥ & ♣.
** With 3 players or more, 9 more Horseman cards: 1-4 & 6-10 in ♦.
** With 5 or 6 players, 9 more Horseman cards: 1-4 & 6-10 in ★.
* 3 Apocalypse cards: the A in ♠, ♥ & ♣, plus ♦A with 3 players or more, and ★A with 5 or 6 players.
* 3 Chaos cards: 3 Wolves (one Wolf per player with 4 to 6 players).
* 3 Tranquility cards: 3 Butterflies (one Butterfly per player with 4 to 6 players).
* Paper and a pencil to keep the score.
****

Apocalypse is a trick-taking game where the goal is to score the fewest points.
Each round, you play cards and influence the Apocalypse cards at the center of the table.
Watch them carefully: if they are all flipped, the rules change and the round descends into chaos!

[CAUTION]
====
The Horseman cards have no 5: only the Chaos cards (Wolves) are worth 5.
The Apocalypse cards are the A cards: they are never dealt nor played, only flipped face down and face up.
====


== Setup

. In a 3 or 4-player game, leave the 9 Horseman cards of ★ in the box.
You will only be using 4 suits.
. Place the Apocalypse cards of the suits used for the game (their A) in the middle of the table, face up.
. The player who most recently rode a horse becomes the first player.


== Game structure

A game takes place over the course of 3 rounds.
Each round is divided into tricks and ends when at least one player has no cards left in their hand.


[#game-flow]
=== Game flow

. Shuffle the Horseman cards and deal a certain number to each player, depending on the number of players:
** 3 players: 10 Horseman cards.
** 4 players: 8 Horseman cards.
** 5 players: 8 Horseman cards.
** 6 players: 7 Horseman cards.
. Give each player 1 Chaos card (a Wolf) and 1 Tranquility card (a Butterfly).

These Horseman and Special cards form each player's hand.
The remaining cards are set aside.
They will not be used this round.


=== Flow of a trick

. Each trick is played clockwise, starting with the first player: they choose any card from their hand and play it on the table, face up.
.. If the card is a Horseman card, its suit becomes the leading suit.
... In turn order, each player must play one card from their hand, face up.
... If a player has one or more Horseman cards of the leading suit, they *must* play one of them (or a Special card, see <<special-cards>>).
However, if a player has no card of the leading suit in their hand, they must play a card of their choice from their hand.
.. If the trick's first card is a Special card (Chaos or Tranquility), the leading suit is not yet determined.
If the 2nd player also plays a Special card, the leading suit will be the suit of the next Horseman card played during the trick (see <<special-cards>>).

IMPORTANT: The leading suit is *always* determined by the first Horseman card played during a trick.


[#apocalypse-cards]
=== Apocalypse cards

When a Horseman card is played (or a Chaos card, see <<special-cards>>), immediately flip the Apocalypse card of the corresponding suit face down.
If this card is already face down, do not flip it.

As soon as all the Apocalypse cards are face down, the current trick becomes an Apocalypse.
After resolving an Apocalypse, flip all the Apocalypse cards face up.


=== Trick resolution

==== Regular trick

The player who played the highest-valued card of the leading suit wins the trick.
They win the card they played and put it face down in front of them.
All the other played cards are put face down in a discard pile.


==== Apocalypse trick

The player who played the *lowest*-valued card of the leading suit wins the trick.
They win *all* the cards played during the trick and place them face down in front of them.


=== Next trick

After resolving the trick, the winner of the trick chooses who becomes the first player to open the following trick.
They may choose themselves as the next first player.


[#end-of-a-round]
== End of a round

If one or more players have no cards left in hand after a trick is resolved, proceed to scoring for the round.

It is possible that one or more players may still have one or two Special cards in hand, because these cards could not be played during the round (see <<special-cards>>).


=== Scoring

* Each Horseman card you win is worth points equal to its face value, from 1 to 10.
* Each Chaos card (Wolf) you win is worth 5 points.
* Tranquility cards (Butterflies) won do not score any points.
* If a player has Special cards remaining in their hand at the end of the round, each of those cards gives them 20 points.


=== Next round preparation

. Collect all Horseman cards, shuffle them and proceed to the round setup (see <<game-flow>>).
. Collect all Special cards and deal 1 Chaos card and 1 Tranquility card to each player.
. The player with the most total points chooses the next first player (they can choose themselves).
In case of a tie for most points, the player who won the last trick chooses the first player.

IMPORTANT: The state of the Apocalypse cards is *not* reset between rounds: if an Apocalypse card is face down, it stays that way.


[#special-cards]
== Special cards

During a trick, the Chaos and Tranquility Special cards can always be played, even if the player has one or more cards of the leading suit in their hand.

However, there can be only *one* Chaos card and *one* Tranquility card in play at any given time during a trick.
A player may, for example, find themselves unable to play if they only have Special cards in hand and these have already been played during this trick.
They must then skip their turn.
Special cards remaining in hand at the end of the round give points to the player (see <<end-of-a-round>>).


=== Chaos card (Wolf)

When a player plays their Chaos card, they *must* flip the Apocalypse card of their choice face down.
If it was the last one, it triggers an Apocalypse.
If all Apocalypse cards are already face down, they do not flip any.

During trick resolution, the Chaos card has a value of 5 and is *always* considered to be of the leading suit.


=== Tranquility card (Butterfly)

This card has *no* value and *no* suit.
A player playing the Tranquility card can never win the current trick.


== End of the game

After the scoring of the 3rd round, the player with the fewest total points wins the game.
In case of a tie, tied players share their victory.


== Example of a trick

====
During a 4-player game, at the beginning of the trick, the ♠A Apocalypse card is face down, and ♥A, ♣A and ♦A are face up.

. Anna opens the trick with ♥8.
Anna immediately flips ♥A face down.
. Barry chooses to play their Chaos card (a Wolf).
They can either flip ♣A or ♦A face down.
They choose to flip ♦A.
. Camilla, fearing that an Apocalypse is about to trigger, chooses to play their Tranquility card (a Butterfly), even though they have a ♥ card in their hand.
. David doesn't have any ♥ cards in their hand.
They choose to play ♣4.
They then flip ♣A, which was the last remaining Apocalypse card, triggering an Apocalypse.

*Trick resolution*

Since it is an Apocalypse trick, the lowest-valued card of the leading suit wins the trick.

Camilla cannot win the trick, since they played their Tranquility card.
David cannot win either, since they played a Horseman card of a suit different from the leading suit.

Barry played a card with a value of 5 in the leading suit (a Chaos card is always considered of the leading suit, even if an Apocalypse card of a different suit is flipped).
Therefore, Barry wins the trick and, since it was an Apocalypse trick, they win all 4 cards that were played in the trick and place them face down in front of them.
These cards will give them 8 + 5 + 0 + 4 points at the end of the round.

All the Apocalypse cards are then flipped face up.
Finally, Barry chooses which player becomes the first player.
They choose Camilla, who will start the next trick.
====


[#duel]
== Duel variant (2 players)

=== Initial setup

. Leave the 9 Horseman cards of ♦ and of ★ in the box, along with ♦A and ★A.
. Shuffle the 27 remaining Horseman cards along with 3 Tranquility cards (Butterflies) and 3 Chaos cards (Wolves).
. Deal 13 cards to each player and set aside the rest of the deck.


=== Duel basics

The regular rules from the base game apply.
The Duel variant differs in how scoring works.
At the end of a round, if a player scored *more than twice* as many points as their opponent, both players swap their scores for the round.
