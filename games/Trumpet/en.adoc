= Trumpet

:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 69 game cards:
** 66 numbered cards: 1-11 in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿ & ★.
** 3 Super-Trumps: J, Q & K in ⎈.
* 6 Trump cards: the A of ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿ & ★.
* 24 path cards:
** 18 empty spaces: the 3 K of ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿ & ★.
** 6 trump spaces: 1-6 in ⎈.
* 2 character cards per player:
** 2 Butterflies & 2 Wolves.
** 2 Phoenixes with 3 players or more.
** 2 Monkeys with 4 players or more.
** 2 Snakes with 5 players or more.
** 2 Dragons with 6 players.

TIP: Instead of the path and character cards, you can play with a link:Trumpet-Board.pdf[board] and player pawns.
****

Each time a player wins a trick, their character moves forward one free space along the path.
When a character reaches a ⎈ space, its owner may choose a trump suit.

The decisions of when to change trumps and when to play a Super-Trump must be made carefully, since they are decisive.
The first player to reach the end of the path wins the game.


== Setup

Build the path in the middle of the table.
Place all the K cards face down: they are the empty spaces of the path.
Place all the ⎈ cards 1-6 face up (*their value does not matter*): they are the trump spaces of the path.

[CAUTION]
====
There is a gap before the last six spaces of the path, to remind the players that these six spaces are special.
This gap is *not* a space of the path.
====

Each player takes a pair of identical character cards, places one in front of them and the other at the start of the path, before its first empty space.

Choose a dealer.
The dealer shuffles the game cards and deals 7 cards face down to each player.
The remaining cards form a face-down draw pile.

Place the 6 Trump cards (A) face down in the middle of the path.


== Playing the game

The player to the left of the dealer leads a card.
Then, clockwise, each player must play a card of the same suit.

If you cannot follow suit, you may play any card.
If you play a trump, you can win the trick; otherwise, your card is lost.

A trick is won by the highest trump or, if there is none, by the highest card of the lead suit.

A Super-Trump beats any other card.
If more than one Super-Trump is played, the last one played wins the trick (the letter of the Super-Trumps *does not matter*).
If a trick is led with a Super-Trump, it has no lead suit: players can play any card they wish.

Once a trick is played out, place its cards face up next to the face-down draw pile.
The player who wins the trick must move their character to the next *unoccupied* space of the path.

NOTE: Only one character can occupy a space.

The player who wins a trick leads the next one.

Once all the cards in hand have been played, the deal passes to the player to the left of the previous dealer.
The game cards are reshuffled and each player is dealt a new hand of 7 cards.


== Trumps

Whenever a character lands on a ⎈ space, its owner may choose a trump suit.
They take the Trump card (A) of the suit of their choice and place it face up at the end of the Trump line, in the middle of the path.

Once all 6 Trump cards are on the Trump line, a character landing on a ⎈ space allows its owner to swap two Trump cards in the line.

If a player has no cards left in hand when their character lands on a ⎈ space, they may wait until the new hand is dealt before choosing trumps.


=== Trump strength

The first Trump card is placed at the beginning of the Trump line.

All trumps are good, but the further along the line its Trump card is, the stronger a trump suit is.
The strongest trump suit beats all the others.
The second strongest trump suit beats all those below it, but not the strongest one, and so on.

A Super-Trump beats all trumps.


== The last six spaces

The last six spaces of the path are special.

When you win a trick while your character is in this region, you have two choices:

* Move your own character forward to the next free space.
* Move the character of an opponent in this same region backward to the preceding free space.

If a character lands on a ⎈ space after moving backward, its owner carries out the normal action of that space.


== Penalties

If you fail to follow suit when you could, you must move your character to the space immediately behind the last character on the path.


== End of the game

The first player to move *past* the last space of the path wins the game.
