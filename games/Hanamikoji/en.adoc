= Hanamikoji

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 7 Geisha cards: ⎈2, ✿2, ♦2, ♣3, ♥3, ♠4 & ★5.
* 21 Item cards:
** K & Q in ⎈, ✿ & ♦;
** K, Q & J in ♣ & ♥;
** K, Q, J & S in ♠;
** K, Q, J, S & C in ★.
* 8 Action cards: 2 Wolves, 2 Snakes, 2 Phoenixes & 2 Dragons.
****

Welcome to the most famous geisha street of the old capital, Hanamikoji.
Geisha, the graceful women who, after years of training, elegantly master art, music, dance and a variety of artistic performances, are greatly respected and adored.
Geisha may be translated as “artist”, and they dance, sing and entertain everyone.

In Hanamikoji, two players compete to earn the favors of the seven geisha masters by collecting the performance items in which they excel.
With careful speculation and sometimes a few bold moves, you may earn the essential items by giving away the less critical ones.
Can you outsmart your opponent and win the most favors of the geisha?

[CAUTION]
====
The value of a Geisha card is its number of Charm Points, and also its number of Item cards.
For the Item cards, only the suit matters: it tells which Geisha the card belongs to.
====


== Aim of the game

In Hanamikoji, the aim is to win the favor of 4 Geisha or to earn 11 (or more) Charm Points.

You and your opponent take turns performing actions to get different kinds of Item cards.
For each Geisha, if you have more Item cards of the corresponding suit than your opponent, you win the Geisha’s favor.

The game continues until a player has reached the winning goal in the Scoring phase.


== Setup

. Place the 7 Geisha cards in a row between the players, in the following order from left to right: ⎈, ✿, ♦, ♣, ♥, ♠, ★.
. Stack the Item cards face down into a pile and place it aside.
. Each player takes 4 Action cards (1 Wolf, 1 Snake, 1 Phoenix & 1 Dragon) and places them face up in front of them.
. The younger player is the starting player.


== Sequence of play

The game is played over *one or several rounds*.
Each round consists of 3 phases, in the given order:

* Phase 1: Deal.
* Phase 2: Action.
* Phase 3: Scoring and update.

If a player reaches the winning goal in the Scoring phase, the game ends immediately.
If neither player reaches the winning goal, the game proceeds to the next round.
The game continues until a player wins.


=== Phase 1: Deal

The starting player shuffles all 21 Item cards into a face-down pile, randomly removes 1 card from the game *without looking at it*, and sets it aside face down.
This card will not be used this round.
*Neither player may look at it.*

Deal 6 Item cards to each player as their hand.
Keep your hand hidden from your opponent.

Stack the remaining Item cards face down as the “Item deck”, and put it next to the row of Geisha cards.


=== Phase 2: Action

Beginning with the starting player, the players alternate *taking one turn (player A → player B → player A → player B → and so forth)* until both players have taken *4 turns*.

On your turn, you must *draw a card* from the Item deck, and then *perform an action*.
*There is no fixed action order.*

To perform an action, choose 1 of your *unused* Action cards and perform the corresponding action.
After resolving the action, turn the card 90 degrees on its side to show that it has been used.
You can’t choose a used Action card (each player uses each of their Action cards only once per round).

There are 4 actions in Hanamikoji:


==== Wolf

Choose 1 card from your hand, and place it *face down* under the Wolf.
This card will be revealed in the Scoring phase and *will be scored*.

You can look at this card at any time.


==== Snake

Choose 2 cards from your hand, and place them *face down* under the Snake.
These cards will *not be scored* this round.

You can look at these cards at any time.


==== Phoenix

Choose 3 cards from your hand, and place them *face up* in front of you.

Your opponent chooses 1 of them, and places this card on their side, next to the *corresponding Geisha*.
Then, place the 2 remaining cards on your side, next to the corresponding Geisha.
*These cards will be scored.*


==== Dragon

Choose 4 cards from your hand, and place them *face up* in front of you.
Divide them into two sets of 2 cards each.

Your opponent chooses 1 set, and places these 2 cards on their side, next to the *corresponding Geisha*.
Then, place the 2 remaining cards on your side, next to the corresponding Geisha.
*These cards will be scored.*


=== Phase 3: Scoring and update

After both players have performed their 4 actions, the game proceeds to Phase 3.

Both players reveal the card under their Wolf, and place it on their side, next to the corresponding Geisha.
Then, compare the number of Item cards on both sides of each Geisha:

* One side has more cards than the other: the side with more Item cards wins this Geisha.
Move the Geisha card a few centimeters towards the winning player’s side.
* Both sides are tied, or have no cards: don’t move the Geisha card.

After scoring, both players count the Geisha they have won and add up their Charm Points.
If a player reaches the winning goal, the game ends immediately (see <<end>>).

If neither player reaches the winning goal, proceed to the update:

* Pick up ALL the Item cards, from the table and the set-aside card, stack them face down into a pile and place it aside.
* The Geisha cards remain in place.
+
NOTE: Don’t move them back to the center of the table.
* Both players turn their Action cards upright again.
* The second player becomes the new starting player.
* The next round is ready to begin.


[#end]
== End of the game

If a player wins 4 Geisha or 11 (or more) Charm Points, the game ends immediately.

If only one player reaches the winning goal, they are the winner.

If one player wins 4 Geisha and the other wins 11 (or more) Charm Points, the latter is the winner.

.Victory
====
[%autowidth, frame=none, grid=none]
|===
| ⎈2 | | | ♣3 | ♥3 | |
| | | ♦2 | | | |
| | ✿2 | | | | ♠4 | ★5
|===

The top player wins 3 Geisha, with a total of 8 Charm Points. +
The bottom player also wins 3 Geisha, with a total of 11 Charm Points.

Since the bottom player reaches the winning goal, the game ends immediately.
The bottom player is the winner.
====
