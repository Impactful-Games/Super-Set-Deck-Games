= Shamans
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* World cards:
** 3 players: 30 cards, 1-6 in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦ & ★.
** 4 players: 36 cards, 1-6 in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿ & ★.
** 5 players: 56 cards, 1-8 in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿, ⎈ & ★.
* 5 Role cards:
** 3 Shamans: ♥K, ♣K & ♦K (♦K with 4 or 5 players only).
** 2 Shadows: ★J & ♠J (♠J with 5 players only).
* 12 Animal cards: 5 Wolves, 3 Phoenixes, 2 Snakes & 2 Dragons.
* The Ø card.
* Moon cards: ★11-17 with 3 players, ★11-19 with 4 players, ★11-19 & ♠10-16 with 5 players.
* 1 Monkey: the first player card.
* Paper and a pencil to keep the victory points.
****

You are a shaman.
Since the dawn of time, you have been protecting the spirit worlds from the threat of the shadows.
Your mission has never been more perilous.
Before the moon is at its zenith, you must restore balance to the different worlds to keep the universe from falling into chaos.

But could the danger come from within your own ranks?
Some shamans, corrupted by the shadows, are secretly working towards your failure...
Perhaps you are one of them?

When the time comes, will you be able to see through the schemes of your rivals and find yourself on the right side of this age-old struggle?


== Concept and goal of the game

Shamans is a trick-taking game that lasts a variable number of rounds.
Each round represents a lunar cycle, during which Shamans and Shadows face off.
In each round, the players are divided secretly and randomly between two teams, the Shamans and the Shadows.
So the teams change from round to round.

* The Shamans win the round if they eliminate all the Shadows, or if the Moon stack is not empty when the players have played all their cards.
* The Shadows win the round, and 3 points each, if the Moon stack is empty.

Players score points when they are on the winning team, and through some effects.
The player with the most victory points at the end of the game wins.


== Setup

* Build the Moon stack and place it face up in the middle of the table, in this order (last card on top):
** 3 players: ★11 to ★17;
** 4 players: ★11 to ★19;
** 5 players: ★11 to ★19, then ♠10 to ♠16.

* Sort the World and Role cards according to the number of players:
+
[%autowidth,cols="^.^,^.^,^.^"]
|===
.2+h| 2+h| Role cards
h| Shamans (K) h| Shadows (J)
h| 3 players | *2* | *1*
h| 4 players | *3* (all) | *1*
h| 5 players | *3* (all) | *2* (all)
|===
+
[%autowidth,cols="^.^,^.^,^.^"]
|===
.2+h| 2+h| World cards
h| Suits h| Values
h| 3 players | 5: *♠, ♥, ♣, ♦ & ★* | *1-6* (30 cards)
h| 4 players | 6: *♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿ & ★* | *1-6* (36 cards)
h| 5 players | 7: *♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿, ⎈ & ★* | *1-8* (all 56 cards)
|===

* With 3 or 4 players, remove the unused cards from the game.

* Give the Monkey, the first player card, to the oldest player, who places it face up in front of them.


== Round setup

. Place all the Moon cards of the game in a face-up stack in the middle of the table.
. Shuffle the 12 Animal cards face down, reveal 2 of them, and place them on the table.
Then shuffle the Ø card, *face up*, with the 10 remaining face-down Animal cards, and place this Animal pile face down next to the 2 revealed Animal cards.
If the Ø card is visible on top of the pile, reshuffle the cards until it is not.
. Deal 1 Role card to each player.
Keep your Role card face down in front of you.
You may look at your Role card at any time.
. Deal out the World cards according to the number of players (9 cards each with 3 or 4 players, 11 cards each with 5 players).
Place the leftover cards (3 cards with 3 players, 1 card with 5 players) face up on the table, sorted by suit.
. The player with the Monkey is the first Guide of the round.


== A turn of play

The Guide chooses a card from their hand and places it face up in front of them.
This card sets the Destination of the turn (the World that the Shamans protect this turn).
Then each other player, in clockwise order, MUST play a card:

* *If your card matches the Destination (a World of the same suit):* +
Place it face up in front of you; it is resolved once everyone has played a card.

* *If your card does not match the Destination (a World of another suit):* +
Immediately discard 1 card from the Moon stack, and place it face down next to the stack.
If the Moon stack is now empty, the Shadows immediately win the round.
+
Place your card on the table, on the pile of the cards of the same suit.
If all the cards of a World are now placed (each World is made of 6 cards with 3 or 4 players, and of 8 cards with 5 players), you must immediately perform the ritual of this World (see <<ritual-effects>>).

NOTE: Unlike many trick-taking games, there are no constraints on the card you play: you may play any card you wish.
You may speak freely about your cards, as long as you do not mention their suits, Worlds or values.


=== Resolving cards

Once everyone has placed a card, check the cards that match the suit of the Destination, to find who played the smallest value and who played the largest value matching the Destination.

NOTE: If you are the only player who played a card *matching the Destination* (or if the other players have been eliminated), you played both the smallest *AND* the largest value.

Then, do the following, strictly in this order:

. *The player who played the smallest value:* +
Take an Animal card of your choice: either one of the 2 revealed ones, or the top card of the pile.
If you take the top card of the pile, you may keep it face down if you wish, unless it is a Snake, which must always be revealed and activated.
If you took a revealed Animal, reveal a new one to replace it.
If, at any moment, the Ø card is visible on top of the pile, immediately apply its effect (see <<zero-card>>).

. *The player who played the largest value:* +
Collect all the cards in front of the players, and place them on the table, sorted by suit, keeping all the values visible.
If all the cards of a World are now placed, you must immediately perform the ritual of this World (see <<ritual-effects>>).
If players still have cards in hand, you become the new Guide and begin a new turn; otherwise, the round ends.


[#zero-card]
==== The Ø card

Each player must pass a card from their hand to their left neighbor (in an even round) or to their right neighbor (in an odd round).
Then discard the Ø card.

NOTE: If the Ø card is revealed in the last turn, some players may have no card in hand.
In this case, only the players with at least one card in hand pass and receive a card.


=== Animal cards' effects

* *Dragon:* +
Keep this card in front of you.
You may discard it at any time, either to discard a card from the Moon stack, or to place a discarded Moon card back on the Moon stack.
+
IMPORTANT: If the Moon stack becomes empty, at any moment whatsoever, the Shadows immediately win the round.
+
You may play your Dragon at absolutely any moment to apply its effect, even just before being eliminated, or just before the Moon stack becomes empty.

* *Snake:* +
If you take this card, you *MUST* immediately reveal YOUR Role card.
Even if you took this card from the top of the pile, you must reveal it immediately.

* *Wolf:* +
Keep this card in front of you.
You may discard it to eliminate a player when you perform a Neutralization ritual (see <<ritual-effects>>).

* *Phoenix:* +
Keep this card in front of you.
At the end of the round, if you have at least two of them and have not been eliminated, you score 2 victory points.


[#ritual-effects]
=== Ritual effects

* *Stabilization ritual (♠):* +
Place 2 discarded Moon cards back on the Moon stack, *OR* take an Animal card (one of the 2 revealed ones, or the top card of the pile).

* *Permutation ritual (♥):* +
Exchange your Role card with that of another player.
Both of you may immediately look at your new Role card.
This might make you switch teams in the middle of the round!

* *Illumination ritual (★):* +
Gain 1 victory point.

* *Neutralization ritual (♣, ♦, ✿ & ⎈):* +
If you have a face-up Wolf in front of you, you *MUST* discard it and choose another player to eliminate for the round (see <<elimination>>).
+
NOTE: If your Wolf is face down, you may decide not to reveal it.
If you have no Wolf, or decide not to reveal one, this ritual has no effect.


[#elimination]
==== Effects of an elimination

The eliminated player reveals their Role card.

* *If the eliminated player is a Shaman:* +
Discard 1 card from the Moon stack for each card the eliminated player still has in hand.
This might make the Shadows win the round.
The eliminated player then sets their cards aside, face down.
These cards will not be placed this round, so some Worlds will not be completed.

* *If the eliminated player is a Shadow:* +
If this was the last Shadow in play, the Shamans immediately win the round.
Otherwise (only in a 5-player game), the eliminated player reveals their hand and places each card on the pile of its World, without discarding Moon cards.
This can trigger a chain reaction if all the cards of one or more Worlds are now placed.
When this happens, the player who eliminated the Shadow resolves the Ritual effects, in the order of their choice.
Then the game goes on as usual.

NOTE: Remove the Animal cards of the eliminated player from play until the end of the round.

IMPORTANT: The card played by an eliminated player is collected and placed as usual at the end of the turn, but its value is ignored when determining the smallest and largest values.
If an eliminated player played the only card matching the Destination this turn, the eliminated player chooses the new Guide.


== End of the round or of the game

The round can end in several ways:

. *When the players have no more cards in hand:* +
The Shamans managed to protect the Spirit Worlds, and win the round.
. *When the last Shadow is eliminated:* +
The Shamans managed to expose all the Shadows, and win the round.
. *When the Moon stack is empty:* +
The Shadows managed to corrupt the Spirit Worlds, and win the round.

Then:

* If the Shamans won the round, each Shaman player who was not eliminated gains 2 victory points.
* If the Shadows won the round, each Shadow player who was not eliminated gains 3 victory points.
* A player who was not eliminated and has 2 (or 3) Phoenixes gains 2 more victory points.
* If nobody has a total of 8 victory points or more, begin a new round.
The player with the Monkey passes it to the player on their left.
You keep nothing but your victory points from one round to the next.
All the players and all the cards are back in the game for the new round.
* As soon as one or more players have a total of at least 8 victory points, the player with the most victory points wins the game.
In case of a tie, play additional rounds until a single player has the most points.

NOTE: The Shadows do not win the round if they eliminate all the Shamans while the Moon stack is not empty at the end of the round.
