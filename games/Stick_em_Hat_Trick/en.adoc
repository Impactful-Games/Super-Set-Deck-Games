= Stick'em “Hat Trick”
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 54 numbered cards: 1-18 in ♠, ♥ & ♣.
* 6 figures: Q & K in ♠, ♥ & ♣.
* With 7 players, 3 more numbered cards: 0 in ♠, ♥ & ♣.
****

Each player tries to collect as many cards as possible in only one suit, and to avoid the cards of the other suits.
The cards of one suit count one point per card.
The cards of the other suits count one negative point per card.
The player with the most points wins.


== Setup

* *With 4 to 6 players*: use all the cards except the 0s.
A player shuffles the cards and deals them out evenly to all players: 15 cards each with four players, 12 cards each with five players, and 10 cards each with six players.
* *With 7 players*: use all the cards.
Each player then gets 9 cards.

[IMPORTANT]
====
* The Q cards are worth *19*.
* The K cards are worth *20*.
====


== Playing the game

The starting player begins by leading a card from their hand to the center of the table.
This begins a trick.
In turn, the other players add a card either of the suit played first, or of a second suit.
With the second suit, a second trick is begun.
Thus, a double trick develops.

IMPORTANT: At most two suits can be played in a double trick.

The player who played the card with the highest value wins the trick.
With a double trick, each suit is won as a separate trick.
The trick winners take their tricks and place their cards in face-up piles, separated by suit.
Thus, everyone sees who has collected which suit.

.Double trick
====
[%autowidth,grid=none,frame=none,cols="^,^,^,^,^"]
|===
| Axel | Lore | Uli | Doris | Birgit
| ♥10  | ♠13  | ♥11 | ♥3    | ♠18
|===
Uli wins the ♥ trick and receives three ♥ cards.
Birgit wins the ♠ trick and receives two ♠ cards.
====

The winner of the previous trick leads the next trick.
If the trick was a double trick, whichever winner won with the highest card leads.
If there is a tie, the player who won the second, third, or fourth highest card leads.
If there is still a tie, it is broken by the suit order: ♠, ♥, ♣.


=== The rules of the tricks

* In a double trick, there may be at most two suits.
* The K, as the highest card, always wins a trick.
* A single card is considered a trick.
* Each player may overbid the highest card or play a lower card.


=== Passing

If a player cannot, or does not wish to, play one of the two suits of the double trick, they must pass.
They must show all the other players any card of the third suit, and put it face down in front of themselves.
Passing is only possible if two suits have already been played in the trick.


=== Scoring

All the cards are played out in successive tricks, up to the final card.

IMPORTANT: The last card is not played.
It is put aside and not scored.

Each player now has up to three face-up piles in front of them, and possibly face-down cards, if they passed during play.
Each player counts their cards of each suit.
The suit of which they have the most cards scores one positive point per card.
If there is a tie between two or three suits, the player chooses one suit to count as positive points.
All the cards of the other suits count one negative point per card.
Face-down cards, from passing during play, count two negative points each, regardless of their suit.

.Scoring
====
Axel has nine ♥ cards, two ♠ cards, four ♣ cards and a face-down card.
Axel's score is stem:[9 - 2 - 4 - 2 = 1] point.
====


=== The next round

After all the scores are tallied, the next round begins.
A player shuffles all the cards together and deals them evenly to all the players, as before.
The player to the left of the dealer leads the first card, and play continues as described before.


== End of the game

The game ends after five rounds.
The player with the most points wins.
If all players have negative points, the player with the fewest negative points wins.
