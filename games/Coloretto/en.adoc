= Coloretto
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 63 color cards: 0-8 in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿, ⎈ & ★.
* 10 "+2" cards: 6 Butterflies & 4 Phoenixes.
* 3 jokers: 3 Wolves.
* The Ø card, to mark the last round.
****

From 2 to 5 players draw cards from a supply in the middle of the table.
During the game, you try to specialize in a few colors, because at the end of the game, only 3 colors score plus points, and all the others score minus points.
So you try to collect cards in three colors and to avoid taking any in the other colors, which is not that easy.
The more cards you have in a color, the more points it scores.
The player with the most points wins.

[CAUTION]
====
For this game, the numbers on the cards *do not* matter.
Only the suit of the cards matters: each suit is one of the seven colors of the game.
====


== Setup

* Choose whether you play with scoring scheme A or B (see <<scoring>>).
+
TIP: Begin with scoring scheme A.
* With 3 players, remove the ★ cards from the game.
* Remove the Ø card from the deck and set it aside for now.
* Each player takes one card of a different color and places it face up in front of them.
* Shuffle the remaining cards and place them face down in the middle of the table as a supply.
Deal 15 cards face down from the supply in a stack.
Place the Ø card on top of this stack, then place the rest of the supply on top of it.
* Choose a starting player at random.

NOTE: The rules below are for 3 to 5 players; the changes for 2 players are explained in <<two-players>>.


== Playing the game

Players take turns clockwise.

On your turn, you *must* take one of the two following actions:

A. Draw and place a card.
B. Take a row.

Then the player on your left takes their turn.

When every player has taken a row, the round ends and a new round begins.


=== A. Draw and place a card

Draw the top card of the supply and place it face up, either next to one or two cards already placed in a row, or alone in a new row.
There can never be more rows than players still in the round.

A row holds *at most* 3 cards.
When a row has 3 cards, you cannot place another card in that row.

When there are as many rows as players still in the round, and each row has 3 cards, you cannot choose this action: you must take a row.


=== B. Take a row

Take all the cards of a row and place them face down in front of you.

You can only take a row that has at least one card.
Once you have taken a row, you take no more turns this round, but you play again in the next round.
The face-down pile in front of you shows everyone that you take no more turns this round.

NOTE: If all players but one have taken a row, this player may keep drawing and placing cards in the last row (up to 3 cards) before taking it and ending the round.


== End of a round

When every player has taken a row, the round ends.
Each player sorts the cards they took by color, face up in front of them.
Butterflies and Phoenixes go in an eighth pile.
Everyone must be able to see how many cards of each color each player has.
Set aside any joker (Wolf) you took: you will choose its color at the end of the game, but not sooner.

A new round begins.
The player who took the last row of the previous round starts the new round.


== End of the game

When the Ø card is drawn, the current round is the last one.
The player who drew it sets it aside, in view of everyone, and draws the next card instead.
At the end of the round, each player chooses the color of each of their jokers (Wolves).
A player's jokers can take different colors.


[#scoring]
== Scoring

Each player counts their cards of each color.

* The table gives the plus or minus points of a color, according to its number of cards.
You score no extra points for more than 6 cards in a color.
+
[%autowidth, cols="^,^,^"]
|===
h| Cards
h| Points +
(scheme A)
h| Points +
(scheme B)

h| 1 | 1 | 1
h| 2 | 3 | 4
h| 3 | 6 | 8
h| 4 | 10 | 7
h| 5 | 15 | 6
h| 6 or more | 21 | 5
h| Each Butterfly or Phoenix | +2 | +2
|===
* Choose *3 of your colors*: they score *plus points*.
* *All your other colors* score *minus points*.
* Each Butterfly and each Phoenix scores 2 plus points.

The player with the most points wins!

.Example (scheme A)
====
Andy has 1 Wolf, 1 Phoenix, 6 ♣ cards, 4 ♦ cards, 3 ♥ cards and 2 ♠ cards.
Andy already scores the maximum with the 6 ♣ cards, so the Wolf goes with the ♦ cards.

♣ = +21 points +
♦ + Wolf = +15 points +
♥ = +6 points +
♠ = -3 points +
Phoenix = +2 points

Andy scores 41 points in total.
====


[#two-players]
== Coloretto for two players

With 2 players, apply the following changes:

* Before starting the game, remove all the ★ and ⎈ cards.
* Before the first turn, each player takes two cards of different colors.
* There are up to three rows, each with its own limit:
** the first row holds at most 1 card;
** the second row holds at most 2 cards;
** the third row holds at most 3 cards.
* When both players have taken a row, the remaining row is removed from the game, with its cards.
