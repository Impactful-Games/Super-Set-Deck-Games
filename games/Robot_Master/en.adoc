= Robot Master
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 36 Robot cards: 0-5 in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿ & ★.
****

[CAUTION]
====
The suits of the cards do not matter.
This game only uses the *values* of the cards.
====

At the beginning of the game, the 2 players, or the 2 teams with 4 players, decide who owns the horizontal rows and who owns the vertical columns.

*Goal*: place the robots of various values to make the most of your rows or columns: at the end of the game, the loser is the player or the team who has the row or column with the lowest score.


== Setup for 2 players

Shuffle the Robot cards and deal 12 cards face down to each player.
Look at your cards without showing them.
Put the remaining cards aside as a draw deck.
Turn the top card of the draw deck face up and put it in the middle of the table.

== Game play

Choose a start player at random.

The start player chooses a card from their hand and places it face up next to the central card, so that it touches a full side of the central card.

Then the other player places a card from their hand.
The card must touch a full side of a card already on the table.
Later, you may place a card that touches several cards already in place.

The game ends when all the cards are placed, forming a 5×5 square.


== Scoring a row or a column

The score of a row or a column is the sum of the values of its robots.

* A single Robot card only counts for its face value.
+
====
5, 0, 2, 3, 1 = 11 points
====

* 2 identical Robot cards count for 10 times the face value of the card.
+
====
3, 0, 3, 1, 4 = 35 points
====
+
NOTE: A row or a column can score 2 pairs of identical cards (2 multiplications).
+
====
5, 2, 2, 1, 5 = 71 points
====

* 3 identical Robot cards count for 100 points, whatever their face value.
+
====
0, 0, 2, 3, 0 = 105 points
====
+
NOTE: A row or a column can score 3 identical cards and a pair of other identical cards.
+
====
4, 4, 2, 4, 2 = 120 points
====

* 4 or 5 identical Robot cards count for 100 points for the first 3 cards, plus the face value of the other ones.
+
====
2, 2, 2, 2, 3 = 105 points
====

[WARNING]
====
* One 0 is worth 0 points.
* Two 0s are worth 0 points.
* Three 0s are worth 100 points!
====


== End of the game

When all the cards have been played to form a 5×5 square, each player calculates the score of each of their rows or columns.
Only the lowest row and the lowest column are compared: the winner is the player who has the higher score on this row or column.

In case of a tie, compare the second lowest row and column.

If you play several rounds, the score of each round is the difference between the lowest row and the lowest column.
The player with the highest total score after several rounds is the winner.


== Variants

=== For 2 players

Play as in the basic rules, but each player receives only 5 cards instead of 12.
Only one card is placed face up in the middle of the table, and the remaining cards are put aside as a draw deck.
Each time you place a card, draw the top card of the draw deck to replace it, so that you always have 5 cards in hand.


=== For 4 players

Play in teams of 2 players.
Team members sit opposite each other.
One team owns the horizontal rows, and the other team owns the vertical columns.
Each player receives 7 cards, and the remaining cards are put aside as a draw deck.

Turn the top card of the draw deck face up and put it in the middle of the table.
One of the players starts the game, then play continues clockwise.
Any communication between team members is forbidden.
Play as in the basic rules.


=== Solo play

Turn only one card face up in the middle of the table, and put all the other cards aside as a draw deck.
Draw and place the cards one at a time (in the same way as in the basic rules).
When the 5×5 square is complete, your score is the score of your lowest row or column.
