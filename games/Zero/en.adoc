= Zero Down
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 56 numbered cards: 1-8 in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿, ⎈ & ★.
* Paper and a pencil to keep the score.
****

In clockwise order, players swap one card from their hand with a card from the pool.
Each player aims to score as few points as possible at the end of the round.
If a player achieves a ZERO (a hand worth zero points), the round ends immediately.
After a number of rounds, the player with the fewest points overall wins.


== Setup

Shuffle the cards and deal 9 cards to each player.
Take your cards in your hand, keeping their values hidden from the other players.
Then place five cards (or, as a variant, seven cards) face up in the middle of the table, next to each other, to form the pool.
With two players, use a pool of nine cards.
Place the remaining cards face down to one side: they are not used in this round.


== Play

The player to the left of the dealer starts the round.
Then play continues clockwise.
On your turn, you must either swap or knock.


=== Swapping

Choose one card from your hand and place it face up in the pool.
Then take a different card from the pool into your hand.
This ends your turn.


=== Knocking

If you do not want to swap a card, knock once on the table.
This ends your turn.

The first knock of the round has no effect: play continues as normal.
However, the second knock of the round (whether by the same player or by another one) announces the last opportunity to swap.
After the second knock, all the players get one more turn before the round ends, except the player who knocked the second time.
Players who do not want to use their last opportunity to swap may simply knock and pass.


== Scoring

Five or more cards of the same suit, or five or more cards of the same number, score 0 points.
Otherwise, each number in your hand scores once.

For example, a single 7 scores 7 points, and so do two, three or four 7s.
However, five 7s or more score 0 points.

.Scoring
====
♣2 ♥5 ★5 ⎈3 ♦3 ♠3 ♣3 ★3 ♥3 -> 2 + 5 = 7 points

✿1 ♠1 ♣4 ♠8 ♥1 ♥3 ♥4 ♥7 ♥8 -> 1 + 4 + 8 = 13 points

♦1 ♠2 ♥2 ♣2 ⎈5 ⎈7 ♠7 ♦7 ★7 -> 1 + 2 + 5 + 7 = 15 points
====


== Zero

If you manage to form a hand of five cards of the same suit and five cards of the same number (with one of your nine cards belonging to both groups), you have achieved a ZERO.
Only then can a hand be worth 0 points.
Immediately reveal your cards and announce “ZERO”.
This ends the round, and all the hands are scored immediately (the other players score their cards normally).

.Zero
====
♠8 ♠7 ♠5 ♠1 ♠2 ♦2 ♥2 ★2 ♣2 -> Zero!
====


== The next round

After writing down the points of each player, shuffle all the cards to start the next round.
Play as many rounds as there are players.

The player with the fewest points overall wins.
