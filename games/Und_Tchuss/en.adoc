= Und Tschüss!
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 90 numbered cards: 1-15 in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿ & ★.
* 8 K: 1 K in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦ & ✿, and 3 K in ★.
* 6 J: J in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿ & ★.
****

A simple, clever game of annoyance for 4 to 6 malicious pleasure seekers aged 10 and up.

It's really very simple.
Points cards lie on the table, and naturally everyone wants to win the biggest one.
However, is your card strong enough?
In each round, one person after the other drops out, and whoever comes second goes away empty-handed.
But whoever knows the right time to drop out, and is sometimes content with poor booty, has played well.
Especially as all the others can take part again in the next round.

[CAUTION]
====
For this game, the suits of the cards do not matter: only their values count.
====


== Setup

Shuffle the cards and deal them as follows:

* With 4 players, 7 cards each.
* With 5 players, 8 cards each.
* With 6 players, 9 cards each.

Lay a few cards face up in a row on the table: one card fewer than the number of players.
These are the points cards being fought over.

[IMPORTANT]
====
The K and J cards are worth negative points:

* each K is worth -5,
* each J is worth -10.
====

Place the remaining cards nearby as a face-down deck.


== Course of the game

The game is played in several rounds.
In each round, the players compete for the points cards on the table.
However, there is always one card fewer than there are players.
Consequently, one player will go away empty-handed.

Each round consists of several stages, in each of which one player drops out and receives the lowest scoring card.
The last and highest scoring card is therefore won by the last player left, who comes first.
The other player, who only comes second, receives nothing at all.


== Playing a round

Each player lays one card face down in front of themselves.
The cards are turned over simultaneously.
Starting with the youngest player, the players announce the values of their cards.
Whoever has played the lowest card drops out of this round.
They take the lowest value points card and put it face down in a scoring pile in front of them.
Their played card goes on a discard pile nearby.

.First stage
====
With 4 players.

Points cards: a K (-5), an 8 and a 12.

Cards played: Peter 5, Claudia 14, Klaus 6, Gaby 6.

Peter has laid the lowest card and must drop out.
Peter takes their leave with a cheerful "So long!", and takes the lowest scoring points card, the K worth -5.
====

All the other players lay a new card face down on top of their previous one, turn them over together again, and add up their two numbers.
Starting with the player to the left of the one who last dropped out, the players announce their new totals.
Whoever has the lowest total drops out, takes the lowest value points card and puts it face down on their scoring pile.
Their cards go on the discard pile.

.Second stage
====
Totals: Claudia 24, Klaus 14, Gaby 15.

Klaus has the lowest total, drops out, takes the lowest points card left (the 8), puts it face down on their scoring pile, and discards their played cards.
====

The game continues like this until only two players are left.
Each again lays a card, giving a new total.
But this time, the winner (the player with the highest total) takes the last points card.
The loser gets nothing.

.Third stage
====
Totals: Claudia 19 (Claudia played a K), Gaby 30 (Gaby played a 15).

Gaby gets the points card worth 12, and Claudia gets nothing.
====


=== Ties

If there is a tie for the lowest total, no player drops out.
The same applies if the last two players tie for the highest total.

In both cases, further cards are laid until the tie is broken.


=== New round, new fortune

The player who came second deals cards from the deck to bring everyone's hand back to the starting number (see the setup).

The player who dropped out first in the round may exchange any number of cards.
They throw their unwanted cards on the discard pile, and take the same number of cards from the dealer to complete their hand.

Points cards (one fewer than the number of players) are laid out on the table again.
The rest of the game proceeds as described.


== End of the game

With 4 players, the game ends when the deck runs out for the first time.

With 5 or 6 players, the game ends when the deck runs out for the second time: shuffle the discard pile to form a new deck after the deck runs out for the first time.

Each player adds up the values of the cards in their scoring pile.
Cards in hand do not count.
Whoever has the most points wins.
