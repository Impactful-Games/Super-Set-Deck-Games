The parade has started in Wonderland.
Everybody is dressed up, the streets are decorated, excitement is in the air.
Let's find more people to join the parade.
You are one of the organizers of the parade, and you are constantly doing your best to encourage people to join it.

If only the people already taking part didn't leave so early...
After a short while, they just lose interest in the parade.
And if somebody shows up with the same costume or of a higher rank, all they do is leave the parade immediately and complain to you.
You just want everybody to have fun, but why is it so difficult nowadays...?


== Goal of the game

On each of your turns, play a card from your hand while trying not to collect cards from the parade, as these score penalty points.
Nobody wants points in Wonderland.


== Preparing the parade

Choose a start player at random.
Starting with the start player, the game continues clockwise.
The start player shuffles the cards and deals 5 cards face down to each player.

Then they place 6 more cards face up next to each other in a line in the middle of the table.
These cards are the first participants of the parade.
The remaining cards form a face-down draw pile, placed at one end of the line to mark the front of the parade.
The other end is called the end of the parade.


== Sequence of play

On your turn, perform the following actions in this order:

. Choose one of the cards in your hand and lay it at the end of the parade.
. If necessary, remove cards from the parade and lay them in front of you.
. Draw a card from the draw pile.

The next player then performs the same actions on their turn.


=== 1. Adding to the parade

Choose one of the cards in your hand and place it face up at the end of the parade.
This card never counts when counting the cards in the next action.


=== 2. Removing from the parade

Depending on the card played, some cards may have to be removed from the parade.

If the number of cards already in the parade is *less than or equal to* the value of the played card, no cards are removed from the parade.

If the number of cards already in the parade is *greater* than the value of the played card, some cards may have to be removed from the parade.

To find these cards, look at the value of the played card.
Then, starting from the end of the parade and ignoring the card you just played, count as many cards as this value.
Every card *beyond* these cards (the cards you did not count) enters *removal* mode.

Then, among the cards in removal mode, remove the following cards:

* All the cards of the same suit as the played card.
* All the cards of a value less than or equal to the value of the played card.

Lay the removed cards face up in front of you, sorted by suit.
The values of all the cards must be visible to all players, so it is best to lay them slightly shifted on top of each other.

The remaining cards of the parade move forward towards the front of the parade, to close the gaps.

If a card with the value 0 is played, *all* the cards of the parade enter *removal* mode.

Since it is a 3 (the value of the card), you ignore the last 3 cards of the parade: they are not affected by the played card.

Here is what happens to the 4 cards in removal mode:

* You take all the cards of the played suit.
Here, ♣0 and ♣7.
* You take all the cards of a value less than or equal to the value of the played card.
Here, ♥3.

Note that only the cards in *removal mode* can be taken.
====


=== 3. Drawing

Draw a card from the draw pile, to have 5 cards in hand again.


== Last round and end of the game

The last round starts when a player has collected at least one card of each of the 6 suits, *or* when the draw pile is exhausted.

If a player collects the 6th suit, they finish their turn as usual.
After that, every player, including the one who collected the 6th suit, plays one more turn.
However, players don't draw a card at the end of their turn.
The game ends after this last round.

If the draw pile is exhausted, every player plays one more turn.
The game ends when everybody has only 4 cards left in hand.

Even if other players collect their 6th suit during this last round, this has no further effect.
The game ends after this last round.


== Scoring

At the end of the game, each player chooses 2 cards from their hand and discards them face down.
Once everyone has discarded, add the 2 remaining cards of your hand to the cards already in front of you.

NOTE: Each of these 2 cards either joins a pile of a suit you already have, or starts a pile of a new suit.

To determine your score, only count the cards in front of you.
Discard the cards still in the parade.

Players determine their score for each suit separately.

. For each suit, find the player who has the most cards of that suit.
The player or players with the most cards of a suit turn their cards of that suit face down.
Each card turned face down is worth 1 point (its printed value does not count).
. Each player then adds up the printed values of all their face-up cards.
Finally, add the points of your face-down cards (1 point per card) to those of your face-up cards.

As you would expect in Wonderland, the player with the fewest points wins the game.
In case of a tie, the tied player with the fewest cards in front of them (face up and face down) wins.

== Two-player game

The rules don't change with only 2 players, except for the scoring.

When you determine the majorities, a player has the majority in a suit only if they have at least 2 more cards of that suit than the other player: these cards then score 1 point each.
If a player has only 1 more card, the same number of cards, or fewer, the values of these cards are added up.
