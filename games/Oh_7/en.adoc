= Oh 7 ‽
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* Base game:
** Game cards: 0-7 in ♠, ♥ & ♣, plus 0-7 in ♦ with 4 players or more, and 0-7 in ✿ with 5 players or more.
** A cards: the A of each suit in play (♠A, ♥A & ♣A, plus ♦A and ✿A).
* _Advanced game_:
** 4 ★ cards: 1-4 in ★.
** 2 Butterflies.
** 1 Monkey.
* Paper and a pencil to write down the bets and the scores.
****

Oh 7 ‽ is played in several rounds of 7 tricks.
You bet the number of tricks that you will win, with one of your own cards.
Will you have the necessary card, or will you need to adapt?

To score as many points as possible, you will not only have to take risks, but also try to make your opponents fail their own bets.


[#setup]
== Setup

Create the deck by shuffling all the game cards of as many suits as there are players:

* 3 players: ♠, ♥ & ♣.
* 4 players: ♠, ♥, ♣ & ♦.
* 5 players: ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦ & ✿.

NOTE: The base game is limited to 5 players, while the <<advanced-game>> allows up to 6 players.

Take a pencil and paper to write down the bets and the scores.


== Round overview

In each round, play the following phases, in order:

1. Deal.
2. Bets.
3. Trick taking.
4. Scoring.

The game usually takes 7 to 9 rounds.


[#distribution]
=== Deal

Place the A cards of the suits in play in a face-down pile in the center of the table.

Deal 7 cards to each player.
Put the remaining cards aside (*the discard*): they won't be used in this round.

TIP: Check that the discard has as many cards as there are players (one card fewer in the <<advanced-game>>).


=== Bets

1. Each player chooses a card from their hand and puts it face down on the table.

2. When everyone has chosen a card, all players reveal it.
For each player, the value of their chosen card is the exact number of tricks that they commit to win during the round (their bet).
+
[IMPORTANT]
====
*The sum of the bets cannot be 7!* +
If it is, restart the Bets phase at step 1, *leaving the previous bet cards on the table*.
Each player must use *another card* to bet.

Only the last chosen card defines a player's bet.

At the second bet, if the sum of the bets is still 7, all players must bet again with a third card.

If, at the third bet, the sum is still 7, restart the round at the <<distribution>> phase by shuffling all the game cards (including the discard).
====

3. Write down the bet of each player on the paper.

4. The first leader is the player who bet the *card with the highest value*.
In case of a tie, the first leader is the tied player who bet with the highest priority suit.
+
IMPORTANT: The suit priority is: ♠ > ♥ > ♣ > ♦ > ✿.

5. The bet card of the first leader defines the *trump suit* for the round.
Take the matching A card, and place it face up on top of the pile of A cards.
It reminds all players of the trump suit during the round.

.Bets
====
Alice bets *♣2*. +
Bob bets *♠3*. +
Carole bets *♠2*. +
David bets *♥0*.

As the sum of the bets is 7, they need to bet again.

Alice bets *♣3*. +
Bob bets *♥3*. +
Carole bets *♥2*. +
David bets *♠0*.

As Alice and Bob have both bet 3, the suit priority decides.
As ♥ has a higher priority than ♣, Bob becomes the first leader, and Bob's card makes ♥ the trump suit for the round.
====


=== Trick taking

This phase is played in 7 tricks.
Each player will therefore play their whole hand.

The first leader leads the first trick.
Each following trick is led by the winner of the previous trick.

Each trick is played as follows:

1. The leader chooses a card from their hand and plays it face up on the table.
The suit of this card is the *lead suit*.

2. In clockwise order, each other player chooses a card from their hand and plays it face up on the table.
+
IMPORTANT: This card *must* be of the lead suit if possible.
If you don't have a card of the lead suit, you can play *any* other card.

3. Determine the winner of the trick:
+
* If there are trump cards in the trick: the player who played *the highest trump card* wins the trick.
* If there is no trump card in the trick: the player who played *the highest card of the lead suit* wins the trick.
+
That player takes the cards of the trick and places them face down in front of them.
If that wasn't the 7th trick, they become the leader of the next trick.
+
CAUTION: Each player should place the tricks they won next to each other, so that *everybody can see the number of tricks won by each player*.
This information is public and always visible.

Once all seven tricks have been played, tally up the scores.


=== Scoring

* Each player who *failed* their bet *loses* as many points as the difference between their bet and the number of tricks they won.
+
NOTE: It is quite possible to have a negative score.
At the end of the first round, at least one player will have a negative score.

The sum of the points lost by the players who failed their bet during this round makes *the basket*.

* Each player who *made exactly their bet* scores:
** 2 points for making their bet,
** + the basket,
** + 1 point for each trick they won.

.Scores
====
Following the previous example, after the trick-taking phase:

Alice won *3* tricks: bet made. +
Bob won *4* tricks: bet failed by stem:[1] trick. +
Carole won no trick: bet failed by stem:[2] tricks. +
David won no trick: bet made.

The basket is therefore made of stem:[1 + 2 = 3] points.

* Players who failed their bet:
** Bob loses stem:[1] point.
** Carole loses stem:[2] points.
* Players who made their bet:
** Alice wins stem:[3 + 2 + 3 = 8] points.
** David wins stem:[3 + 2 + 0 = 5] points.
====

The game is played in a *minimum* of 7 rounds.
However, it is not possible to win the game with a last failure.

From the end of the 7^th^ round on, the player with the highest score wins the game *if they made their bet during the last round*.
If the player with the highest score did not make their bet during the last round, additional rounds are played until a winner can be designated.

To win the game, a player must therefore:

* have played at least 7 rounds,
* have made their bet during the last round,
* have the highest score.

In case of a tie for the highest score:

* If at least one of the tied players failed their bet, an additional round is played (the leader must be overtaken).
* If all the tied players made their bet, the player who made the most bets wins the game. +
If there is still a tie, the tied players share the victory.
+
NOTE: Players can agree beforehand that all ties trigger an additional round.


[#advanced-game]
== Advanced game

*For 3 to 6 players.*

Play with one suit fewer than the number of players:

* 3 players: ♠ & ♥.
* 4 players: ♠, ♥ & ♣.
* 5 players: ♠, ♥, ♣ & ♦.
* 6 players: ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦ & ✿.

Add the *special cards*:

* *The 1-4 in ★*:
** In the bet phase: +
The player of a ★ card cannot become the first leader (even if they played the card with the highest value).
To determine the first leader, ignore the ★ cards.
The ★ suit will therefore never be trump.
** In the trick-taking phase: +
The ★ cards are considered of the lead suit. +
The ★ cards are weaker than the lead suit cards of the same value.
For example, if ♥ is the lead suit, the order is ♥2 > ★2 > ♥1.
* *The 2 Butterflies*:
** In the bet phase: +
A Butterfly is a bet of 0 tricks.
If the bet is made, the player *does not score* the 2 points for their bet: they only score the basket.
** In the trick-taking phase: +
A Butterfly is lower than any other card: it can never win a trick.
* *The Monkey*:
** In the bet phase: +
The Monkey *cannot be used to bet*. +
However, in case of a tie for the highest bet, you can show that you have the Monkey in your hand to win the tie (instead of using the suit priority).
** In the trick-taking phase: +
The Monkey is stronger than any non-trump card, but weaker than any trump card.

[CAUTION]
====
* Special cards can be played at any time, whether you have cards of the lead suit or not.

* If a player leads a special card, the lead suit is defined by the next player.
If they also play a special card, the lead suit is defined by the next player, and so on. +
If a trick only contains special cards, the strongest card is the Monkey, followed by the ★ cards, then the Butterflies.
====


== Two-player game

Create the deck as if you were playing with three players.

The two players face each other, and a third player, the Dummy, is simulated.

Deal a hand of 7 cards to each player.
Put the remaining cards in a pile between the players: this is the *Dummy's pile*.

During the bet phase, the Dummy always bets 2.
Therefore, if the sum of the players' bets plus 2 is 7, they need to bet again.
The Dummy never becomes the first leader (its bet is ignored for that purpose).

After the bet phase, before starting the first trick, reveal 2 cards of the Dummy's pile and place them face up next to it.

The Dummy always plays last (after both players), unless it won the previous trick, in which case it leads the trick, followed by the first leader.

The Dummy tries to win exactly 2 tricks.

Each time the Dummy plays last, reveal a third card from its pile and play one of the three revealed cards on its behalf:

* *If it wants to win the trick* (because it has won fewer than 2 tricks):
** *If it can win the trick*:
*** *If it is its first trick*: it plays the lowest card that wins the trick.
*** *If it is its second trick*: it plays the highest card that wins the trick.
** *If it cannot win the trick*: it plays the lowest possible card.
* *If it wants to lose the trick* (because it has already won 2 tricks):
** *If it can lose the trick*: it plays the highest card that loses the trick.
** *If it cannot lose the trick*: it plays the highest possible card.

In case of a tie between non-trump cards for the highest or lowest card, it follows the suit priority.

If the Dummy plays first (because it won the previous trick), it plays the top card of its pile (its 2 revealed cards stay on the table).

Do not keep scores for the Dummy.
