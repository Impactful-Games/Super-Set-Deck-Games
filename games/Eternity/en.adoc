= Eternity
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 42 Game cards: 1-14 in ♠, ♥ & ♣.
* 3 Trump cards: the A of ♠, ♥ & ♣.
* 18 Animal cards: 6 Butterflies, 6 Wolves, 4 Phoenixes & 2 Dragons.
* Paper and a pencil to keep the score.
****

Score points by matching the tricks you take with the pledges you make.
To do so, you have to:

* collect the animals you pledge to shelter;
* conquer the peninsulas (the tricks) in which to shelter your animals.

CAUTION: For this game, all the Animal cards are equivalent: the kind of animal does not matter.


== Setup

On the side of the table, place the 3 Trump cards side by side, in the order of your choice.
These 3 cards form the *trump board*.
Leave room under each card for a column of cards of its suit.

Next to it, place the 18 Animal cards in a face-up pile.

*A game is played in 3 rounds.*

*The wisest player* (who owns this Super-Set Deck) is the first dealer and the first player.


== Playing the first round

The dealer shuffles the Game cards face down and deals them two by two:

* with 5 players, 8 cards to each player;
* with 4 players, 10 cards to each player.

Place the 2 remaining cards on the trump board, in the column of their suit.

* With 3 players, deal as with 4 players, then place the 10 cards that were not dealt face up above the trump board.

The number of cards in hand determines the number of turns (the number of tricks) of the round.

On the trump board, the trump suit is the suit with the most cards, or, in case of a tie, the leftmost of the tied suits.
The values of the cards on the trump board do not matter.

[IMPORTANT]
====
*In Eternity, the trump suit can change at the end of each turn!*
Watch the trump board carefully.
====


=== Game turn

To start a turn (a trick), the first player plays a card.

Each following player then chooses to:

* *Take part in the trick:* play a card of the suit of the first card.
If you have none, you must play a card of the trump suit.
If you have none either, play any card.

* *Pledge:* *do not put your card in the trick*, but *place it face up and sideways in front of you*.
You may pledge a card of any suit, since the pledge card is not part of the trick.
Then take a number of Animal cards that depends on the value of the pledge card, and place them face up in front of you:
+
[%autowidth,cols="^.^,^.^,^.^,^.^,^.^,^.^,^.^,^.^"]
|===
h| 1 .4+| *0* .5+| h| 5 .5+| *1* .5+| h| 10 .5+| *2*
h| 2 h| 6 h| 11
h| 3 h| 7 h| 12
h| 4 h| 8 h| 13
2+| h| 9 h| 14
|===
+
If the card gives 0 animals (values 1 to 4), you take none, but your pledge still counts.

[IMPORTANT]
====
In a turn (a trick), with 3 or 4 players, only one player may pledge.
With 5 players, only two players may pledge.

So the other players must take part in the trick.
A trick may also have no pledge at all.
====


=== End of the turn

When every player has played a card, the turn is over.

*Among the cards that take part in the trick:*

* if there are trump cards, the highest trump card wins the trick;
* if there is no trump card, the highest card of the suit led (the suit of the first card of the trick) wins the trick.

The player who played the winning card takes the trick: they stack all the cards of the trick face down in front of them.
This player has conquered a peninsula!
Peninsulas cannot be turned back face up to check their cards.

*The pledge card* (or the two pledge cards with 5 players) *is added to the trump board*, in the column of its suit.
The players then find out the trump suit of the next turn.

The player who won the trick becomes the first player of the next trick.
Turns follow one another until the players have no more cards in hand.


=== Sheltering an animal

During the round, as you get tricks and animals, you may shelter your animals.
To do so, place one of your Animal cards face up on one of your tricks: the animal is sheltered.
You may always place one of your animals on one of your tricks at any time, whichever of the two you got first.


== End of the first round

Each player checks their tricks and their animals:

* If you have exactly as many animals as tricks, *you score 1 point per animal, plus the harmony bonus*: *2 points* in this first round.
* If all your animals are sheltered, BUT you also have tricks without animals, *you score 1 point per animal*.
* If you have animals without shelter, *you score no points!*
Such is the fate of those who leave animals without shelter and do not honour their pledges!

IMPORTANT: A player who has taken no animals and no tricks has as many animals as tricks, and scores the harmony bonus too.

Write down the points of each player.
All the players then return their Animal cards next to the trump board.


== Second and third rounds

These two rounds are played as the first one, except that:

* *The player who scored the fewest points* in the previous round shuffles all the Game cards (those of the tricks and of the trump board), deals and leads the first trick.
In case of a tie, the oldest of the tied players starts.
* *The harmony bonus is 4 points in the second round, and 7 points in the third round!*
(Remember that a player with 0 tricks and 0 animals scores it too.)


== End of the game

After three rounds, the player with the highest total score wins the game.
In case of a tie, the tied player who scored the most points in the last round wins.
If there is still a tie, the tied players share the victory.


== First past the post variant

You may play longer games, over more rounds, up to a set number of points (20, for instance).
In this case, the normal rules apply, except that:

* the *harmony bonus* is always *3 points*, at the end of each round;
* *only the player (or the players, in case of a tie) who scores the most points in the round* scores these points (animals and harmony bonus).
The other players score nothing for the round.


== Two-player game

The dealer shuffles the Game cards face down and deals *14 cards* to each of the two players.
The 14 remaining cards form a face-down neutral pile, which acts as a third player.

Each round, brief and intense, is played in *7 tricks*.

At the start of each turn, reveal the top two cards of the neutral pile.
Beware: in the first turn only, place these two cards on the trump board at once.

In each turn, the two players (A and B) play the four cards of the trick in this order: a card from A, a card from B, a card from B, a card from A.
*Otherwise, the normal rules apply in full.*

* The first player of a turn may pledge when they play their second card, since their first card opens the trick.
* The second player may pledge with either their first or their second card.

Of course, there is only one pledge per trick.
