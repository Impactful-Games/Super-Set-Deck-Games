= Kobayakawa
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 27 play cards: 0-21, J, S, C, Q & K in ★ (fewer with fewer players, see <<setup>>).
* 1 first player card: the Monkey.
* 64 coins (tokens, or face-down cards that are not ★).
****

Players try to win coins with the highest card in their hand, but the Kobayakawa card can always change the outcome.
The goal of the game is to have the most coins at the end of the last round.


[#setup]
== Setup

Select the play cards according to the number of players:

* 3 players: 0-11.
* 4 players: 0-14.
* 5 players: 0-17.
* 6 players: 0-20.
* 7 players: 0-21, J & S.
* 8 players: 0-21, J, S, C, Q & K.

[IMPORTANT]
====
With 7 or 8 players, the J, S, C, Q and K cards have these values:

[%autowidth,cols=">,^,^,^,^,^"]
|===
| Card | J | S | C | Q | K
| Value | 22 | 23 | 24 | 25 | 26
|===
====

*Everyone starts with 5 coins*, and the youngest player takes the Monkey: they are the first player.


== Steps of a round

Each round is divided into two phases: the draw phase and the bet phase.


=== Draw phase

. *Give 1 coin* from the bank to each player.
(Everyone's coins must always be visible to everyone.)
. Starting with the first player and going clockwise, *deal 1 card* face down to each player from the shuffled deck, then put the deck face down on the table.
(Players are only allowed to see their own card.)
. Deal 1 card face up next to the deck: this is the *_Kobayakawa_ card*.
At the end of the round, its value is always added to the card with the lowest value.
. Starting with the first player, everyone *must* do one of these actions, in clockwise order:
.. Draw the top card of the deck and *secretly choose 1* of your two cards to keep in play.
The other card is discarded: put it face up in front of you, so that the players can see which card is out of play.
.. Draw the top card of the deck and put it face up next to the deck: this is the *new value* of the _Kobayakawa_ card.
(Put the new card down in a way that the value of the old card can still be seen.)


=== Bet phase

. Starting with the first player, everyone must choose one of these *betting* options, in clockwise order:
.. *Place a bet* with coins from your supply: this creates a prize pool for the winner of the round.
The maximum a player can bet is the round number + 2 (maximum 3 coins in the first round, maximum 7 coins in the fifth round).
After the first player, the following bets must be equal or higher.
When nobody wants to raise their bet anymore (in clockwise order), the prize pool is ready.
If a player cannot place enough coins to match the agreed bet, they can still stay in play by putting all their coins in the prize pool.
.. *Pass* to stay out of the round, even without placing a bet.
When a player passes, the coins they placed in the prize pool cannot be withdrawn.
They place the card in their hand face down in front of them.

If only one player has not passed, that player wins the round, takes the prize pool and, as a bonus, as many coins from the bank as the number of completed rounds (for example, 4 coins at the end of the fourth round).

If 2 or more players are still in play:

[start=2]
. Each player who passed *must predict* the winner of the round, as long as they have at least 1 coin, by announcing the chosen player.
They place a bet of 1, 2 or 3 coins on their own face-down card.
These coins are not part of the prize pool, and the players who passed do not have to agree on their bets.
. It is finally time to *reveal the cards* of everyone in play!
Find the player who has the card with the lowest value, and add the value of the _Kobayakawa_ card to it.
The winner of the round is the player with the highest total.
In case of a tie, the player with the highest card in hand wins.
. The winner of the round takes the prize pool and, as a bonus, as many coins from the bank as the number of completed rounds (for example, 4 coins at the end of the fourth round).
The players who predicted the winner correctly get their bets back, plus the same amount from the bank.
The players whose prediction was wrong put their bets into the bank.

Finally, give the Monkey to the winner of the round: they are the first player of the next round.
Get ready to start a new round!


== End of the game

*After 5 rounds*, the player with the most coins wins the game.
In case of a tie, the tied players share the victory.

At the beginning of the game, players can agree to play 3 or 7 rounds instead.


== Meta-Kobayakawa

You can play several games of Kobayakawa in a row, for a grand evening of betting and bluffing!

After each game, award:

* 1 point to the winner of the game.
* 1 point to the player who came in second place.
* 1 point to the winner of the last round of the game.

Play until one player has reached:

* 7 points with 3 players.
* 6 points with 4 players.
* 5 points with 5 players.
* 4 points with 6 players.
