= Trio
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 36 cards: 1-12 in ♠, ♥ & ♣.
****

Win trios (a trio is made up of 3 cards of the same number) before your opponents.

* Simple mode:
** Either *any* 3 trios.
** Or the *7 trio*.

* Spicy mode:
** Either *2 connected trios* (among the trios you won) *whose sum or difference is 7*.
+
====
For example, winning the 2 and 5 trios, or the 2 and 9 trios, earns victory.
====
** Or the *7 trio*.

[CAUTION]
====
For this game, the suits of the cards do not matter.
Only their numbers count.
====


== Individual rules

=== Preparation

Shuffle and deal the cards face down to each player according to the table below.
Then put the remaining cards face down in the middle of the table, next to each other.

[%autowidth, cols="^,^,^"]
|===
| Players | Cards per player | Cards in the middle

h| 3 | 9 | 9
h| 4 | 7 | 8
h| 5 | 6 | 6
h| 6 | 5 | 6
|===

* Each player picks up their cards and sorts them in numerical order, from lowest to highest, without showing them to anyone.
* Decide whether you are playing in simple or spicy mode.
* The player who ate avocado most recently goes first.


=== The game

Play in turn, clockwise.
On your turn, reveal numbers one by one: you must stop as soon as 2 numbers are different, otherwise continue until 3 identical numbers are revealed.

For each number, you can choose to reveal a card:

A. *In the middle of the table*: turn any card you like face up in the middle of the table.
B. *From a player's hand, including your own*: reveal a player's *lowest* or *highest* number, either from your own hand or by asking an opponent.
Place the card face up on the table.

Your turn ends in one of two ways:

* Either you complete a trio, and seal the deal by taking the 3 cards face up in front of you.
* Or a revealed number is different from the previous one, and all the revealed numbers are put back in place: face down in the middle, and/or back to their owners.

In both cases, it is the next player's turn.

[IMPORTANT]
====
* *You can perform the exact same action several times*.
For example: ask the same opponent to reveal their highest number, then ask them again for the highest of their remaining numbers.
* Even when you reveal a card from your own hand, *it must be your lowest or highest number* (*never* a number from the middle of your hand).
* If you have no cards left in your hand on your turn, you still play, using the cards in the middle and your opponents' cards.
====

.Example turns
====
Thomas asks Nick for their lowest number (a 3), then asks Isabella for their lowest number (a 2).
Thomas's turn ends immediately.
The 2 different cards go back to their owners, and it is the next player's turn.

Isabella turns over a card in the middle (a 2), then reveals their own lowest number (a 2), and then reveals their lowest number again (a 2).
Isabella therefore wins the 2 trio, and it is the next player's turn.
====


=== End of the game

A player claims victory as soon as they have won:

* 3 trios or 2 connected trios (whose sum or difference is 7), depending on the game mode (simple or spicy);
* or the 7 trio (which triumphs over everything else!).


== Team rules (with 4 or 6 players)

Make the following changes to the rules above.


=== Preparation

* Form teams of 2 players: 2 teams with 4 players, and 3 teams with 6 players.
Sit opposite your partner, not side by side.
* Shuffle and deal *all* the cards face down.
There are no cards in the middle of the table.
* After sorting your cards, you can *swap* one with your partner.
All the teams can do this at the same time.

.Swapping
[NOTE]
====
If 2 partners wish to swap cards, each of them secretly selects one of their cards; they exchange them face down at the same time, and discreetly put them in the right place in their hand.

Swapping is the only form of communication between partners.
All other forms of communication are prohibited (eye signals, kicking under the table, showing where a swapped card was placed…).
====


=== The game

* Each time a team wins a trio, the opposing teams can do a new *swap*.
* The 2 partners share the trios they won.
We recommend putting them together in front of one of the two players.

.Team turn
====
Thomas asks Jade for their highest number (a 10), then asks their partner Isabella for their highest number (a 10), and reveals their own highest number (a 10).
So their team wins this trio, and the other 2 teams can do a swap.
====


=== End of the game

A team claims victory as soon as it has won:

* 3 trios or 2 connected trios (whose sum or difference is 7), depending on the game mode (simple or spicy);
* or the 7 trio!
