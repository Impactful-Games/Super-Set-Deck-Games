= Stick'em Classic
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* Up to 114 cards, depending on the number of players (see <<setup>>): 0-15, J, Q & K in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿ & ★.
* Paper and a pencil to keep the score.
****

Each player is dealt cards.
Each player selects one of these cards to set their own "misery colour".
The goal of the game is to take as many tricks as possible, while avoiding the cards of your misery colour.
The cards of your misery colour bring you negative points, while the other colours bring positive points.
The player with the most points wins.

CAUTION: In this game, a colour is a suit, whatever the colour printed on the cards.


[#setup]
== Setup

The cards used in the game depend on the number of players.
The cards not listed are not used.

* *3 players*: 0-8 in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦ & ✿.
* *4 players*: 0-11 in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦ & ✿.
* *5 players*: 0-14 in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦ & ✿.
* *6 players*: 0-14 in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿ & ★.
* *7 players*: 0-15, J & Q in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿ & ★.
* *8 players*: 0-15, J, Q & K in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿ & ★.

Shuffle the cards and deal them out to the players.
Each player receives 15 cards (14 cards with 8 players).

[NOTE]
====
With 7 or 8 players, some cards remain undealt: 3 cards with 7 players, 2 cards with 8 players.
Show these cards to all the players, then set them aside.
====


=== Choosing your misery colour

Look at your hand and choose your misery colour for this round.
Then select a card of this colour from your hand and place it face down in front of you.
Once everyone has placed a card, all the players reveal them at the same time.
These cards stay face up until the end of the round, so that everyone knows everyone else's misery colour.
Several players may choose the same misery colour.

Each card of your misery colour that you take in tricks scores its value in negative points.
The misery begins with the choice of the misery colour, because the card you used to declare it counts against you at the end of the round.

[TIP]
====
When choosing your misery colour, do not choose a colour in which you have only 1 to 3 cards, nor a colour in which you have very many cards.
Also, it is better to choose a colour in which your cards have low values, so as not to take tricks with high cards of your misery colour.
====


== Playing the game

The starting player begins by playing a card in the middle of the table.
Then, in turn, each player must play a card from their hand.
You do not have to follow suit.
Any card can be played.

The cards played in the middle of the table form the "trick".
The colour of the first card played is the "lead colour".
Every other colour played in this trick is a trump colour.
Every card of a trump colour is a trump card, except the 0 cards.

CAUTION: The 0 cards are not trump cards.


=== Who wins a trick?

IMPORTANT: With 7 or 8 players, the J cards are worth 16, the Q cards 17 and the K cards 18.

* If only cards of the lead colour were played in the trick, the player who played the highest card wins the trick.
* If, besides the cards of the lead colour, only 0 cards were played, the player who played the highest card of the lead colour wins the trick.
A 0 card is not a trump card.
* If, besides the cards of the lead colour, at least one trump card was played, the player who played the highest trump card wins the trick.
A 0 card cannot win this trick.
* If several trump cards share the highest value, the player who played the first of them wins the trick.
* If only 0 cards were played in the trick, the player who led the first 0 card wins the trick.

The winner of the trick takes the cards and places them face down in a pile in front of them.
This player then leads the next trick by playing a card.
The colour of this card is the new lead colour.


=== End of the round and scoring

The round ends after 14 tricks (13 tricks with 8 players).
Each player now has at least one card of their misery colour (the card used to declare it), and possibly other cards taken in tricks.
Each card of your misery colour scores its value in negative points.
Each card of another colour scores 1 positive point, whatever its value.
To find your score for the round, add your positive points and subtract your negative points.

IMPORTANT: With 7 or 8 players, the J cards are worth 16, the Q cards 17 and the K cards 18.

.Scoring
====
Axel used ♥4 to declare ♥ as misery colour.
In tricks, Axel took ♥2 and ♥5, as well as 14 cards of other colours.
Axel scores 14 points for the 14 other cards, and stem:[4 + 2 + 5 = 11] negative points for the ♥ cards.
So Axel's score for the round is stem:[14 - 11 = 3] points.
====


=== The next round

Once every player's score is noted, the next round begins.
The player with the lowest score shuffles the cards (including those set aside in the previous deal, with 7 or 8 players) and deals them (setting aside the undealt cards again).
If several players are tied for the lowest score, the player who scored the least in the previous round deals.
Each player chooses a misery colour, which may or may not be the same as in the previous round.

The player to the left of the dealer leads the first trick, and play continues as described above.


== End of the game

The game ends after five rounds.
The player with the highest positive score wins.
If all players have negative points, the player with the fewest negative points wins.


== Variants

=== "Reverse" Stick'em

The players select a personal "lucky colour" from the cards in their hand.
Each card of your lucky colour that you win in tricks scores its value in positive points.
Of course, the card chosen at the beginning to declare your lucky colour also counts in your favour.
However, each card of another colour now scores 1 negative point, whatever its value.

[IMPORTANT]
====
The lowest trump card wins the trick, rather than the highest.
A 0 card still cannot win a trick.
If several players play a trump card of the lowest value, the first player who played such a card wins the trick.
====

Otherwise, play with the rules of Stick'em Classic.


=== "Mild" Stick'em

Each card of your misery colour scores 5 negative points, rather than its value.
This also applies to the 0 card of that colour.
Each card of another colour still scores 1 positive point.

Otherwise, play with the rules of Stick'em Classic.


=== "Aggravated" Stick'em

Each player chooses two personal misery colours, and selects two cards from their hand.
Each card of your two misery colours scores its value in negative points, and each card of another colour scores 1 positive point.

So only 13 tricks are played with 3 to 7 players, and only 12 tricks with 8 players.

Otherwise, play with the rules of Stick'em Classic.


=== "Hazy" Stick'em

The card declaring each player's misery colour stays face down in front of them.
It is only revealed at the end of the round.

Otherwise, play with the rules of Stick'em Classic.


=== "Delayed" Stick'em

The players select their misery colour not at the beginning of the round, but at the end, when counting the cards won in tricks.
Each player must choose as misery colour a colour among the cards they won.
If you have not won any card of a colour, you cannot choose that colour as your misery colour.

A player who did not take any trick scores 0 points.

In this variant, 15 tricks are played (14 tricks with 8 players).

Otherwise, play with the rules of Stick'em Classic.
