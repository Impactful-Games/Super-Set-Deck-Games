= Ultimatch
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 48 numbered cards: 1-8 in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦, ✿ & ⎈.
****

Work together as a group to remove all the cards of the pyramid, by matching their suit, their number, or both, with cards from your hand and/or from the shared alta-hand.

[NOTE]
====
♠, ♥ and ♣ are the primary suits.
♦, ✿ and ⎈ are the secondary suits, each made of two primary suits:

* ♦ = ♠ + ♣,
* ✿ = ♠ + ♥,
* ⎈ = ♥ + ♣.
====


== Setup

1. Shuffle the cards and deal them to form a pyramid in the middle of the play area.
The number of rows of the pyramid depends on the number of players, as shown below.
+
--
* *1 to 3 players:* 5 rows (15 cards in total).
* *4 or 5 players:* 6 rows (21 cards in total).
--
+
The cards of all the rows except the last one are face down.
The cards of the last row are face up.
Each card overlaps part of the card(s) above it, as shown in the example below.
+
.▒ = face down; U = face up
----
            ╭───╮
            │ ▒ │
         ╭──┴╮ ╭┴──╮
         │ ▒ ├─┤ ▒ │
      ╭──┴╮ ╭┴─┴╮ ╭┴──╮
      │ ▒ ├─┤ ▒ ├─┤ ▒ │
   ╭──┴╮ ╭┴─┴╮ ╭┴─┴╮ ╭┴──╮
   │ ▒ ├─┤ ▒ ├─┤ ▒ ├─┤ ▒ │
╭──┴╮ ╭┴─┴╮ ╭┴─┴╮ ╭┴─┴╮ ╭┴──╮
│   ├─┤   ├─┤   ├─┤   ├─┤   │
│ U │ │ U │ │ U │ │ U │ │ U │
╰───╯ ╰───╯ ╰───╯ ╰───╯ ╰───╯
----

2. Deal each player a hand of cards according to the number of players, as shown below.
+
* *1 player:* 13 cards.
* *2 players:* 6 cards.
* *3 players:* 4 cards.
* *4 players:* 5 cards.
* *5 players:* 4 cards.

3. Place the remaining cards face down to form a draw pile.

4. Each player donates a card from their hand to the alta-hand.
The alta-hand is a hand of cards that every player may use to make matches with the cards of the pyramid.
All the donated cards remain face down until everyone has placed a card in the alta-hand.
Then turn them over to reveal the cards of the alta-hand.
These cards remain face up for the rest of the game.
*In a 2- or 3-player game, also draw the top card of the draw pile and add it to the alta-hand.*


== How to play

The player who donated the highest number to the alta-hand starts.
In case of a tie, the tied player closest to the dealer’s left starts.
On your turn, follow this order of play:

1. Trade a card.
2. Make a match.
3. Remove the matched cards.
4. Reveal pyramid cards.
5. Get the Ultimatch rewards.

You may discuss how to use the cards of the alta-hand to match the cards of the pyramid, but you cannot discuss or show the cards in your own hand.


=== Trade a card

All the players look at their hands to see if they have a card that could help the current player.
If they do, they hold the card face down toward the current player.
Trades are silent.
You may indicate the strength or the nature of your card with facial expressions, body language, gestures, or the way you place the card in front of you.
The current player may choose 1 player to trade with, and give them 1 of their cards face down in exchange.
The current player may not look at the offered card until the trade is made.
Both players then add the card they received to their hand.
(A player who has no cards can still receive a card instead of trading.)


=== Make a match

Play 1 or 2 cards from your hand and/or from the alta-hand to match 1 of the face-up cards of the pyramid.
There are several ways to *make a match*:

* Play 1 card that has:
** the same number,
** or the same suit.

*OR*

* Play 2 cards (no more) that have:
** numbers that *add up* to the number of a face-up pyramid card (any suits),
** or numbers whose *difference* is the number of a face-up pyramid card (any suits),
** or primary suits that mix into the secondary suit of a face-up pyramid card (any numbers).
+
TIP: ♦ = ♠ + ♣, ✿ = ♠ + ♥ and ⎈ = ♥ + ♣.

You must make a match on your turn if you can.
However, if the only possible matches use only cards of the alta-hand, you may pass.

*Ultimatch!*
If you match both the suit AND the number of a face-up pyramid card, you have made an Ultimatch!
At the end of your turn, you may then draw 2 cards from the draw pile, adding 1 to your hand and 1 to the alta-hand.
(You can make an Ultimatch on a card of a secondary suit with 2 cards of that same secondary suit, or with 2 cards of the primary suits that mix into it.
In either case, the numbers of the cards must add up or subtract to the number of the pyramid card.)

NOTE: Primary suits mix the same way, whether you add or subtract the numbers.

*If you cannot make a match, you must pass.*
*Draw 1 card and end your turn.*
*If all the players pass one after another, the game ends in a loss.*
If the draw pile is ever empty and a player needs to draw a card, shuffle the discard pile to make a new draw pile.


=== Remove the matched cards

Place the cards played to make the match and the matched pyramid card face up in the discard pile, next to the draw pile.


=== Reveal pyramid cards

If cards of the pyramid no longer have any card overlapping them, turn them face up: they can be matched by the next players.


=== Get the Ultimatch rewards

If you made an Ultimatch in step 2, draw 2 cards from the draw pile.
Choose 1 to keep in your hand, and place the other face up in the alta-hand.

Play continues with the player to your left, following the same order of play.


== End of the game

The game ends in a win if the last card of the pyramid is matched.
If all the players pass one after another, the game ends in a loss.


== Solitaire

Form a 5-row pyramid and deal yourself a hand of 13 cards.
There is no alta-hand, no trading and no passing in solo mode.

On your turn:

1. Make a match.
2. Remove the matched cards.
3. Reveal pyramid cards.
4. Get the Ultimatch rewards (and keep both cards in your hand).

If you cannot make a match, you lose.
If you match the last card of the pyramid, you win.


== Difficulty

For an *easier* game, add 1 or 2 cards of the draw pile to the starting alta-hand, or allow the current player to make 2 trades.

For *more challenge*, randomly remove 1 card from the starting alta-hand, or keep both cards of an Ultimatch reward in your hand (none added to the alta-hand).
