= Egocentric World
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 60 numbered cards: 1-12 in ♠, ♥, ♣, ♦ & ★.
* Paper and a pencil to keep the score of a game in several rounds.
****

Each player controls a city, represented by their hand of cards.
Each card has an *Egocentrism* value, and sometimes a *special ability*.

*The goal of each player is to finish the game with the lowest Egocentrism score*.
However, cards with a high Egocentrism give advantages during the game.
A game is made of one or several *rounds*.

[CAUTION]
====
For this game, the suits of the cards *do not* matter.
Only the values of the cards matter.
====


== Setup

At the beginning of each round, shuffle the *60 cards* and form a face-down *deck* in the center of the table.
Flip the top card to start a *discard pile*.

At the beginning of each round, *deal 5 cards to each player*: this is their hand.
You may not look at the cards of the other players.

When the deck is empty, shuffle the discard pile and place it face down to form a new deck.

Players take turns clockwise.

In the first round, pick the first player randomly.
In each following round, the first player is the one who had the highest score at the end of the previous round.
Break ties randomly.


== Discarding or playing a card

A card put on the discard pile is *discarded* when it comes directly from the deck, or *played* when it comes from the hand of a player.

A *discarded* card is put face up on the discard pile *without triggering its special ability*.

A *played* card is put face up on the discard pile, and the player who played it *may trigger its special ability*.

On your turn, *you must perform exactly one of the following actions*:

* *Draw* the top card of the deck and look at it secretly.
Then, EITHER *discard* it, OR keep it to replace one card of your hand.
The replaced card is then *played*.

* *Take the top card of the discard pile* to replace one card of your hand.
The replaced card is then *played*.

* *Reveal a card of your hand* with the same value as the top card of the discard pile, and *play* it.

* *Reveal a pair of cards with the same value* from your hand, and *play* one of the two.
The other one stays in your hand.

* *Say "STOP"*: the round will end just before your next turn starts.

A round ends immediately when a player has no more cards in hand.

When a round ends, *each player adds up the Egocentrism values of their hand*.
*The player with the lowest score wins*.

In a game of several rounds, each player adds up their scores of every round.
Rounds are played until a player reaches *50*.
Then, the player with the lowest total wins.


=== Special abilities

When a card with a value from 8 to 12 is *played*, the player who plays it decides whether its special ability is triggered or not.

* *12*: *You may play another turn* after this one.

* *11*: *Designate two players* (you may be one of them) and *exchange two cards from their hands*.
In the hand of each of these players, you may *either take a card secretly* (chosen freely in your own hand, or *picked randomly* in the hand of another player), *or announce a specific value*.
If the player has a card with this value, they must exchange it.
If you are wrong for one or both players, the exchange is cancelled, and you must draw a card and add it to your hand as a penalty.

* *10*: *Designate another player and look secretly at all the cards in their hand.*

* *9*: This special ability is not triggered when the card is played.
*As soon as another player designates you* to resolve the special ability *10* or *11*, you may reveal and *play* this card immediately to *cancel the effect of this special ability*.

* *8*: During your turn, when this card is in your hand or on top of the discard pile, *you may use it as if its value were 7, 8 or 9*.


== Solo mode

Instead of other players, *you face two Citybots*, one on your left and one on your right, which count as players for the rules.
All the usual rules apply, with the following exceptions:

* For each game, *choose a difficulty level: 1, 2 or 3*.
At the end of each round, before calculating the scores, at difficulty 2, each Citybot removes its card with the lowest value.
At difficulty 3, each Citybot removes its card with the highest value.
* In the first round, the first player is the Citybot on your left.
* The hand of a Citybot is made of *a pile of face-down cards*, 5 cards at the beginning of a round, and of *visible cards*, revealed during the game.
The cards a Citybot gets during the game are added to its visible cards.


=== Citybot's turn

To resolve *the turn of a Citybot*, go through the following steps in order, until its turn ends:

. If all its cards are visible and their *Egocentrism* total is lower than yours, the Citybot says "*STOP*" and its turn ends.
. If not all its cards are visible, it *reveals* the top card of its pile.
. If not all its cards are visible and none of its visible cards has a value higher than *7*, it *reveals* the next card of its pile.
. If one of its visible cards has the same value as the top card of the discard pile, the Citybot *plays* this card and its turn ends.
. If several of its visible cards have the same value, the Citybot *plays* one of them (the highest value if several pairs are visible) and its turn ends.
. If the highest value among its visible cards is higher than the value of the top card of the discard pile, the Citybot takes this card, *plays* its card with the highest value, and its turn ends.
. If its turn has not ended yet, the Citybot *draws* a card from the deck and *reveals* it.
. If the highest value among its visible cards is higher than the value of the drawn card, the Citybot takes the drawn card, *plays* its card with the highest value, and its turn ends.
. In any other case, the drawn card is *discarded* and the Citybot's turn ends.


=== Citybots' special abilities

*When a Citybot plays a card with a value from 8 to 12*, the following rules apply:

* *12*: *The Citybot takes another turn* after this one.

* *11*: *The Citybot reveals the top card of its pile and exchanges it with your card with the lowest value*.
If all its cards are visible, the Citybot exchanges its card with the highest value with your card with the lowest value.

* *10*: *All the cards still hidden in the pile of the other Citybot are revealed*.

* *9*: *You cannot use the special abilities 10 or 11 against a Citybot with a visible 9 card*.
If the special ability *10* reveals one or more 9 cards in the pile of a Citybot, this Citybot immediately *plays* one of these cards.

* *8*: During steps 4 and 5 of a Citybot's turn, when this card is among its visible cards or on top of the discard pile, *it counts as having the values 7, 8 and 9*.
If the Citybot can play a 7 or a 9 card during step 4, it does not use this special ability.


=== Your special abilities

*When you play a card with a value from 8 to 12*, the following rules apply:

* *12*: *You may play another turn* after this one.

* *11*: *Exchange one of your cards with a card of a Citybot*: one of its visible cards, or the top card of its pile (the card you give it stays visible).
*OR exchange two visible cards of two different Citybots*.

* *10*: *Reveal all the cards still hidden in the pile of the Citybot of your choice*.

* *9*: This special ability is not triggered when the card is played.
*As soon as a Citybot uses the special ability 11*, you may immediately *play* this card to *cancel this special ability*.

* *8*: During your turn, when this card is in your hand or on top of the discard pile, *you may use it as if its value were 7, 8 or 9*.
