= Jekyll vs Hyde
:toc: preamble
:toclevels: 4
:icons: font

[.ssd-components]
.Components
****
* 21 Evil cards, in 3 colors: 1-7 in ♠, ♥ & ♣.
* 4 ★ cards: 2-5 in ★.
* 3 A cards, to rank the colors: ♠A, ♥A & ♣A.
* 3 Order cards: 1-3 in ♦.
* Paper and a pencil to note Mr. Hyde's points.
****

Jekyll vs. Hyde is a trick-taking game for 2 players, based on the famous novella Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde by Robert Louis Stevenson.
As Dr. Jekyll, you need to resist Mr. Hyde's fierce attacks to protect your mind and keep the secret of your dual nature.
As Mr. Hyde, your goal is to dominate Dr. Jekyll to overcome the doctor's identity.

One player is Dr. Jekyll; the other is Mr. Hyde.
The game plays in three rounds.
At the end of each round, Dr. Jekyll's identity progressively disappears under Mr. Hyde's relentless attacks.

Mr. Hyde wins the game upon reaching 5 points.
If Mr. Hyde has not reached 5 points after three rounds, Dr. Jekyll wins, escaping from Mr. Hyde's dark influence.


== Setup

. Choose who is going to be Dr. Jekyll and who is going to be Mr. Hyde.
. Mr. Hyde starts the game with 0 points: note them on a sheet of paper.
. Place the three Order cards in order (♦1, ♦2, ♦3) between the two players, and the three A cards face down nearby.
. Shuffle all the Evil cards and ★ cards together.
. Mr. Hyde is the dealer, and Dr. Jekyll is the starting player.


== How to play

The game plays over 3 rounds.
In each round, play the following phases in order:

. Preparation
. Trick-taking
. Progression of Evil


=== 1. Preparation

Each player receives 10 cards (the 5 remaining cards are not dealt: set them aside).

Each player then chooses 1 card from their hand to exchange with their opponent.
Place the card face down on the table and exchange them simultaneously (in round 2, you exchange 2 cards, and in round 3, 3 cards).

IMPORTANT: If you receive 2 or more ★ cards among your 10 starting cards, you must include at least 1 ★ card in the exchange.

.Round 2 example
====
It is the beginning of round 2.
Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde must exchange 2 cards each.

Dr. Jekyll has 3 ★ cards and must include at least 1 of them in the exchange.
Mr. Hyde has only 1 ★ card and does not have to exchange it.
====


=== 2. Trick-taking

At the beginning of each round, after the preparation phase, Mr. Hyde's points determine who is the starting player.
If Mr. Hyde has 0, 1 or 2 points, Dr. Jekyll starts the round.
If Mr. Hyde has 3 points or more, Mr. Hyde starts the round.
So Dr. Jekyll always starts the first round.

*There are 10 tricks per round, and each trick plays as follows:*

* The starting player plays any card from their hand, face up.
* If the starting player plays an Evil card, their opponent must play a card of the same color, or they may play a ★ card instead.
If they have no card of this color, they may play any other card.
* If the starting player plays a ★ card, they must announce a color.
Their opponent must play a card of this color *(even if they have a ★ card!)*.
If they have none, they may play any other card.

*Then, compare both cards:*

* If both cards are of the same color, the highest number wins.
* If the cards are of different colors, the highest color wins (see <<color-rank>>), regardless of the numbers.
* If a ★ card was played, resolve its effect first, then the highest number wins.

The winner takes both cards of the trick and becomes the new starting player.

.What is trick-taking?
[TIP]
====
Trick-taking is a type of game in which each player plays a card on the table.
The player who played the best card takes them all.
The cards that have been played form a trick, and the winner takes it.
====


[#color-rank]
==== Color rank

Colors are not ranked at the beginning of the game: in each round, you define their rank by playing cards.

The first color to appear (first Evil card played) automatically has the lowest rank.
Immediately place its A card below the ♦1 Order card.
When the second color appears, place its A card below ♦2.
From then on, the color that has not been played yet automatically becomes the best color of the round, and its A card goes below ♦3.
★ cards have no color and cannot be ranked.

.Example
====
. Dr. Jekyll plays ♠6.
This is the first Evil card of the round, so ♠ takes the lowest rank, and ♠A goes below ♦1.
. Mr. Hyde has no ♠ card in hand, and so plays ♥4.
♥A immediately goes below ♦2, and Mr. Hyde wins this trick, even though Dr. Jekyll's card has a higher number, because the color outranks the number and ♥ ranks higher than ♠.
. Automatically, ♣A goes below ♦3.
====


[#star-cards]
==== ★ cards

When a player plays a ★ card, its effect depends on the card played by their opponent:

* ♠ card: the winner of the trick takes one trick (two cards) from their opponent.
* ♣ card: both players choose 2 cards from their hand (or 1 if it is the last one) and exchange them simultaneously.
* ♥ card: remove the A cards from the Order cards and put them back nearby, face down.
The color rank is reset: the next color to appear will be the lowest one, and so on.

[NOTE]
====
* Whatever happens, when a ★ card is played, the highest number always wins the trick.
* Double ★: if both players play a ★ card, they cancel each other out.
Nothing happens, and the highest number wins the trick.
====

CAUTION: A ★ card beats the other cards of the same value: for example, ★2 beats any other 2, but not a 3.

.Example
====
As the starting player, Dr. Jekyll plays ★4 and announces ♥.
Mr. Hyde must play a ♥ card, and plays ♥6.
The effect of the ★ card resets the color rank, and Mr. Hyde, who played the higher number, wins the trick.
====


=== 3. Progression of Evil

Compare the number of tricks each player won in this round, subtract the lower number from the higher one, and divide the result by 2.
For instance, if Dr. Jekyll won 6 tricks and Mr. Hyde won 4, the result is stem:[(6 - 4) / 2 = 1].
Mr. Hyde gains this number of points for the round.

[NOTE]
====
Evil is relentless!
Even if Dr. Jekyll won more tricks than Mr. Hyde, Mr. Hyde still gains points.
As Dr. Jekyll, your goal is to keep the balance, without giving in to darkness!
====

If Mr. Hyde now has 5 points or more, Mr. Hyde instantly wins the game.
Otherwise, start a new round: remove the three A cards from the Order cards, and reshuffle all the cards, including the 5 cards set aside at the beginning of this round.


== End of the game

At the end of the third round, Dr. Jekyll wins if Mr. Hyde has fewer than 5 points.


== Expert variant: return match

In the base game, Dr. Jekyll is under constant pressure and must plan every action with care.
For a more balanced and tactical game, play 2 games and switch roles between them.

At the end of each game, Mr. Hyde scores as many points as Mr. Hyde has gained, up to 5 points.
The best Mr. Hyde after two games wins the match.
If both Mr. Hydes scored 5 points, the one who took fewer rounds to do it wins.
If this is not enough to break the tie, the match ends in a tie.
